THE TILTED GENT

Vegas after midnight, worn by a gentleman. A neon-tube playing card in a violet-black room, gold for the hardware, marquee pink and vacancy cyan for the tubes around it. One mark, one palette, three typefaces — everything on the site is built from what's on this page.

01 · The Mark

The TG monogram card.

An interlocked gold T and G on a playing card tilted about 8.6° off level, a spade pip in the corner, a crimson accent inside the G. The frame is a gold neon tube with a thinner red tube inside it. Three cuts of the same drawing, each for a different job.

02 · Palette

Fremont Street: same gold, louder room.

The room is near-black with a touch of violet in it, not grey and not red. Gold is the brand and is lifted straight from the monogram. Marquee pink is the emphasis tube; vacancy cyan is the interactive tube; ultraviolet lives only in the ground. Green and crimson are data colours. Tokens are CSS custom properties on every chrome page, with a reference copy in assets/palettes/fremont-street.css. The earlier black, gold and crimson palette is archived beside it as original-black-gold-crimson.css.

03 · Typography

Roman caps, clean body, monospace odds.

Cinzel matches the Roman capitals cut into the monogram, so the wordmark and the logo are the same voice. DM Sans carries the reading. JetBrains Mono is for anything that looks like a number, a label or a ticker. Playfair Display is retired from the chrome pages; it lives on only inside the 189 report documents.

04 · Wordmark

The tilt is the point.

In running text the wordmark is set in Cinzel 700, uppercase, Gent Gold, tracked wide. The word TILTED is an inline block rotated 5° anticlockwise and lifted one pixel, in Neon Gold. That rotation is the joke and the brand. Keep it.

DO

  • Set TILTED as inline-block, rotate(−5deg) translateY(−1px), transform-origin 50% 60%, colour Neon Gold.
  • Keep the marks on dark backgrounds. Black, Back Room, or a dark gradient.
  • Pair the mark with the wordmark at nav sizes; let the lockup stand alone at hero sizes.
  • Give the mark clear space of at least a quarter of its width on every side.

DON'T

  • Straighten the card, straighten TILTED, or italicise it instead of rotating it.
  • Put the neon marks on white or light backgrounds. Use the favicon cut, which carries its own black square.
  • Recolour the mark, drop the red tube, or swap the spade for another suit.
  • Use Playfair, or any serif other than Cinzel, for the wordmark.

06 · Voice

Story first. Math second. Lecture never.

A gentleman who has lost money at both tables and kept his sense of humour. He explains, he doesn't preach.

The Lens

Poker and probability are how every idea gets explained. A company is a hand history, a position is a bet, a drawdown is variance. If it can't be said in odds, it isn't finished.

The Lines

Open with the story, land the number, leave the moral to the reader. Short sentences. Dry wit over jokes. "Be wrong half the time and still profit" is the thesis; everything else is arithmetic.

The Rules

Never name or show a gambling operator. Every page carries the disclaimer. Casino math is taught as the cost of fun, never as a system. Education and entertainment, not advice.
