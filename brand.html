03Logo Concepts

A · The Chip & Topper

RECOMMENDED PRIMARY

A poker chip is the brand's whole thesis in one object — quantified risk, chips as bankroll units. The top hat tilts 12° off level (a gent slightly on tilt), gold band and monocle keep him dressed. Reads at any size, works as a favicon, a chip face, or an embroidered patch.

BEST FOR  site header, channel avatar, merch (actual chips), stamps/seals

B · The TG Monogram

COMPACT / SECONDARY

The tilted T against an upright gold G — one letter on tilt, one composed, which is roughly the whole curriculum. Four card-suit pips replace the chip's edge spots at the compass points. This is the small-space workhorse: watermark bug, favicon, chip inlay, cufflinks if we're feeling fancy.

BEST FOR  favicon, social profile at small sizes, video corner bug, merch details

C · The Gent

PERSONALITY / VIDEO

The invisible man in the red-lit room: tilted topper, gold aviators catching the table light (straight from your degen artwork), crimson bow tie. No face — every viewer gets to be the gent. This is the mark with the most character, and the natural mascot for thumbnails and Le Degens of the Game.

BEST FOR  YouTube thumbnails, video intros/outros, memes, stickers & tees
