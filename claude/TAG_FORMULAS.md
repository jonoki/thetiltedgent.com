# Tag formulas: "what kind of stock" (style tags)

Generated by `py -3 tools/style_tags.py` → `data/style_tags.json` (every input with its raw page text, every threshold, every tag with its tooltip). Re-run after any batch of builds or refreshes. Decisions: Oki, 22 Sep 2026.

## Universe and method

- **Thresholds** come from **S&P 500 members only**, meaning cards on `reports/index.html` with `data-sp`: 500 reports as of 22 Sep 2026. They are then applied to **every** report, including Nasdaq-100, Dow and global names outside the S&P. That is why a "top 20%" tag can cover more than 20% of the library.
- **Rank-based tags** use the top or bottom **20%** (`PCT = 0.20`). The quantile is linear interpolation over the sorted values: `q(p) = v[⌊k⌋] + (v[⌈k⌉] − v[⌊k⌋])·(k − ⌊k⌋)`, where `k = (n − 1)·p`.
- **Each report is tagged on its own as-of numbers.** Reports carry different dates (7 Aug – 22 Sep 2026), so thresholds are a blend of those dates. Tooltips always state the as-of date.
- **Percentile in tooltips:** `rank = 100 × (# S&P values strictly below x) / n`. "Cheaper than N%" uses `100 − rank`.

## Inputs and where they come from

| Input | Source | Parsing |
|---|---|---|
| price, 52-week high, market cap, EPS, trailing P/E, yield, FCF | manifest shard record (`data/reports/*.json`) | first number in the text; `n/m`, `n/a` → none; parentheses or a leading minus → negative; T/B/M/K suffixes. If the manifest has no P/E, the table row `Trailing P/E` / `P/E (Trailing)` is used instead. |
| revenue growth, ROIC, debt-to-equity, beta | the report's own `.fin-table` rows `Revenue Growth…`, `ROIC`, `Debt-to-Equity`, `Beta…`, copied as text into the manifest record's `fin_table` (since 26 Sep 2026; run manifest.py first) | same number parser |
| S&P membership, industry | `reports/index.html` card (`data-sp`, `.sect`) via `tools/manifest.parse_index_cards` | — |
| exclusions by industry | card industry label | Quality: `BANK`, `INSURANCE`, `REIT`. Cash machine: `BANK`, `INSURANCE`, `CAPITAL MARKETS`. |

FCF quoted in another currency (¥, €, NT$, C$…) or as a bare number is ignored, because it can't be divided by a US-dollar market cap. This affects SONY, TM, TSM, SAP, SPOT, CNI, CNQ, CP and FISV.

## Why these choices (decided 22 Sep 2026)

- **Growth uses revenue, not the ratio of trailing to forward P/E.** That ratio mixes GAAP trailing EPS with adjusted forward EPS: CRWD's trailing P/E of 6,234 against a forward 176 implied +3,400% growth. Revenue growth is on one basis for every company.
- **Value has no growth condition** (Oki): it is simply the cheapest 20%.
- **Quality uses ROIC, not ROE.** Buybacks and leverage inflate ROE (APP 204%). Banks, insurers and REITs are excluded because ROIC doesn't describe a balance-sheet business (Oki).
- **Quality has no multi-year check.** It passes cycle-peak names on one year of ROIC: MU, WDC, SNDK, NEM. Accepted for now.
- **The Value tooltip quotes the S&P median P/E** (Oki). The plain mean (49.7) is distorted by P/Es in the hundreds.

## Known data issues (fix in the reports or manifest, not in the formulas)

- **Messy manifest P/E and EPS:** 100 records store them as text with notes ("53.0× (GAAP)", "~257x", "83.2 n/m"). The parser copes; the manifest should carry a clean number plus a basis field.
- **Profitable but no P/E** (they get no Value tag): BA, BN, NBIS, VTR.
- **Mixed revenue-growth basis:** some reports quote trailing twelve months, some the last fiscal year, some the last quarter year-on-year. The raw text is kept in `raw.revg`.
- **Beta basis varies** by report (5-year monthly for most). LMT's 0.11 looks low and needs checking.
- **Extreme values** (real, but worth a look): revenue growth NBIS +454%, SNDK +175%, MU +167%; ROIC PLTR ~364%, VEEV 364%, NTAP 313% (flagged ⚠ on the page), ANET 285%, EXPE 257%; debt-to-equity CLX 22.2, LVS 17.0, CL 13.9 (low or negative equity).
- **SONY is tagged Not yet profitable.** Its consolidated TTM EPS is −$0.24/ADS because of a ¥1,357.8B spin-off loss. The tag is correct by rule; the tooltip quotes the EPS.
- **Manifest duplicates:** AWK, CINF, CPAY, FFIV, Q and WRB also appear in `data/reports/unclassified.json` without their S&P flag. The script keeps the sector copy.

- **CBOE/MTD titles (fixed 22 Sep):** `cboe_analysis.html` carried the `<title>` "PCG — PG&E" and `mtd_analysis.html` carried "CBOE — Cboe". The report bodies were correct. The manifest read tickers from the titles, so its CBOE/MTD records were mislabelled until the titles were fixed (b62d9e6).

## S&P membership audit (22 Sep 2026, against Wikipedia's constituent list, 503 tickers)

- **Every S&P member's card carries `data-sp`.** The apparent gaps (CINF, CPAY, FFIV, Q, WRB) were the manifest duplicates above.
- **S&P tickers without their own report:** GOOG, FOX and NWS are second share classes, covered by GOOGL, FOXA and NWSA.
- **FI → FISV:** Fiserv moved to Nasdaq as FISV on 11 Nov 2025. The report was renamed on 22 Sep 2026 (`fisv_analysis.html`); `view.html?r=fi` redirects.
- **Join-date differences between our cards and Wikipedia:** CBOE is correct at 2017-03-01 per the S&P DJI release of 27 Feb 2017. CHTR, GEN, NWSA, PNW, UHS and ZTS differ by days or weeks within the same year. Cards show the year only, so none change what readers see.
