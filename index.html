The Gent Himself

Twenty years of tuition,
paid at both tables.

I answered a classified ad and became a prop trader. I sat down at a poker table and became a student of variance. Fifteen-plus years trading and investing, twenty-plus playing cards, and a CFA charter later, I noticed something: the people blowing up in markets and the people blowing up at casinos were making the exact same mistakes — with different vocabulary.

The Tilted Gent is the course I wish someone had handed me before either table took my money. Story first, math second, lecture never. Be wrong half the time and still profit — that's not a slogan, it's an expected-value calculation, and I'll show you the arithmetic.
