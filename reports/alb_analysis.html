01 Company Overview

Albemarle is a lithium producer first: brine from Chile's Salar de Atacama and Silver Peak, Nevada, hard rock from its Australian interests, and conversion plants that turn both into battery-grade LCE for cathode makers. Behind the lithium sits a steadier bromine-and-specialties business. The company was spun out of Ethyl Corporation in February 1994, bought Rockwood Holdings in January 2015 in a cash-and-stock deal valued at about $6.2B, and joined the S&P 500 on July 1, 2016.

This is a commodity story, and the income statement shows it. Revenue went from $3.33B in FY2021 to $9.62B in FY2023 as lithium prices spiked, then fell to $5.14B in FY2025 when they collapsed. Diluted EPS went from $22.84 in FY2022 to −$11.20 in FY2024. In Q3 2026 it realised $20.02/kg of LCE, up 94.7% on a year earlier ($10.28/kg), and its 2026 guidance treats ~$10/kg as the low case. That price move is almost all of the 2026 turnaround: Q3 2026 net sales $1.553B (+18.8%), adjusted EBITDA $645.5M (+186.1%), diluted EPS $2.15 against −$1.72 a year earlier. Over nine months, net sales were $4.725B against $3.715B and adjusted EBITDA $2.167B against $0.829B. The stock ran with it, from a $55.76 month-end low in May 2025 to $196.70 in April 2026, a 3.5x move. Since then it has given back about half, to $99.25.

So you are not really buying a chemical company. You are buying a bet on the lithium price with a balance sheet attached, and that balance sheet is in much better shape than it was a year ago. Management's own 2026 guidance shows how much hangs on that one price: at about $10/kg it guides to $0.9–1.0B of adjusted EBITDA, and at about $30/kg to $4.2–4.4B. Midpoint to midpoint that is about 4.5x, on a single input.

03 Competitive Moat & Vulnerabilities

Sources of moat:

Atacama brine (Chile) Australian hard-rock interests (Talison, Wodgina) Silver Peak, Nevada (US lithium brine) Conversion know-how Bromine franchise Long-term customer agreements

In a commodity business, the moat is position on the cost curve, not pricing power: when prices collapse, the higher-cost producers idle first. The recent losses show the limits of whatever advantage it has: at 2025-level prices even Albemarle idled capacity (Kemerton Train 1) and reported negative GAAP EPS in FY2024 and FY2025. The Specialties bromine business is the steadier half. It grew both price and volume in Q3 2026, but it is a quarter of sales.

Key Vulnerabilities

01
It takes the market price on a volatile commodity. Management's own 2026 guide spans $0.9B to $4.4B of adjusted EBITDA depending on the lithium price. No operational decision the company makes moves the result as much as that one input.
02
Chinese supply can come back fast. Tickeron, July 8, 2026: Chinese lithium carbonate prices fell to a ten-week low in late June as traders braced for a possible restart of CATL's Jianxiawo mine, and the stock fell with them. Supply decisions in China, not demand in the US, set the price everyone else gets.
03
Chile. Albemarle's Atacama brine is in Chile, where in April 2023 President Boric announced that control of the country's lithium operations would move over time to a state-owned company, with talks starting through state miner Codelco (Reuters, April 25, 2023). Albemarle's contract to operate the Atacama salt flat runs to 2043. Terms set by the Chilean government on its best asset are a risk that does not go away.
04
Dilution from the mandatory convertible. The Q3 release puts adjusted diluted shares at 137.1M, which assumes conversion of the mandatory convertible preferred (a security that turns into common shares on a set date). That compares with 118.01M common shares outstanding per StockAnalysis, about 16% more shares by our arithmetic. Preferred dividends were $41.7M in Q3 alone.

08 Summary & Key Risks

Bull Case

At $99.25 you pay about 9.4x consensus 2026 EPS of $10.56 (StockAnalysis, read November 9) and about 4.7x the midpoint of the ~$20/kg adjusted EBITDA guide ($11.74B enterprise value ÷ $2.5B, our calc.). That is for a company that has already cut debt by $1.3B, sold its non-core catalyst businesses, has more than $150M of run-rate cost savings and generated $325M of free cash flow in Q3 alone. Leverage is 0.3x. If lithium holds near Q3's $20.02/kg, the market is pricing in a relapse that has not happened in the numbers. If it moves toward the ~$30/kg 2021–25 average, guided EBITDA rises by roughly 70% from the ~$20/kg midpoint. Fourteen of 23 analysts rate it a Buy, and the highest target on the board is $211.

Bear Case

Almost every good number in 2026 is a price number, and the price is set in China. The stock fell 23.5% in June as Chinese lithium prices slid on fears of new supply. At ~$10/kg, management's low case, management's own guide is $0.9–1.0B of EBITDA, and 9.4x forward earnings becomes something far higher. Management also expects Q4 Energy Storage sales and EBITDA to fall from Q3 on lower pricing. Trailing GAAP EPS is $4.37, flattered by three strong quarters. There is roughly 16% dilution waiting in the mandatory convertible, the best asset sits under terms set by the Chilean government, and the CEO is changing in February. Consensus FY2027 EPS is already 17.6% below FY2026.

CRITICAL CONSTRAINT

Albemarle's 2026 guidance spells out the problem: three scenarios at roughly $10, $20 and $30 per kilogram of LCE, producing net sales from $4.1B to $7.8B. The company does not forecast the price. It shows you what each price is worth. In late June 2026, Tickeron reported, Chinese lithium carbonate prices fell to a ten-week low as traders braced for CATL's Jianxiawo mine to restart, and sentiment toward lithium stocks turned sharply worse. Long-term supply agreements dampen the swings but do not remove them.

Self-help while the price does the heavy lifting
IN PROGRESS

Management has done what a company that cannot set its own selling price can do. It sold control of Ketjen and exited Eurecat, retired $1.3B of debt, idled Kemerton Train 1, cut 2026 capex guidance to $300–350M from about $500M, and reports more than $150M of run-rate cost savings so far this year. These steps lower the price at which Albemarle breaks even. They do not change the price it receives. The board's choice of a BHP commercial chief as the next CEO fits that direction.

Valuation depends on the lithium price you assume
OPEN QUESTION

On trailing GAAP earnings ALB trades at 22.7x. On forward numbers it looks cheaper (9.4x FY2026 consensus, ~4.7x the ~$20/kg EBITDA midpoint, our calc.). The two describe different lithium prices. The bull framing: the balance sheet and cost base are now built to survive $10/kg and profit heavily at $20. The bear framing: $20 is the anomaly, not the norm, and Chinese supply will make sure of it. A reader should form a view on the lithium price first. The stock valuation follows from it.
