01 Company Overview

Axon Enterprise sells conducted energy weapons (CEWs — handheld devices that deliver an incapacitating electrical current, sold under the TASER brand), body-worn cameras, in-car video, drones and counter-drone systems to police departments, and then sells those same departments the cloud software that stores, indexes and manages the evidence those devices produce. FY2025 revenue was $2,779.5M, up 33% from $2,082.5M in FY2024. Q2 2026 revenue was $904M, up 35% year over year — the company's tenth consecutive quarter of 30%+ growth. Axon is classified by data providers under Industrials / Aerospace & Defense and ranks roughly #609 globally by market capitalization.

The framing that matters for this business: Axon began life as TASER International, a hardware company, and the hardware is still there — but the economics have migrated. Software & Services was $397.8M of Q2 2026 revenue at a 71.3% gross margin, against 51.9% for the entire Connected Devices hardware segment. The devices are increasingly the razor; EVIDENCE.COM and the bundled software plans are the blades. Sections 02 and 04 quantify that split rather than asserting it.

EVIDENCE.COM is the cloud repository where body-camera footage, in-car video, digital case files and chain-of-custody records live. Around it Axon sells tiered software bundles — the AI Era Plan, Axon 911 (dispatch/CAD), and Axon Fusus (real-time crime centre integration) — which are the named drivers of the segment's 36% growth. Segment gross margin was 71.3% in Q2 2026, and management stated the software-only margin exceeded 80%. That margin fell from 75.6% a year earlier, because the fast-growing bundles carry embedded hardware and third-party AI inference cost; the adjusted figure was 75.1%, down 380 bps. The lock-in mechanism is specific rather than rhetorical: a department's evidence archive is a set of legally sensitive records subject to statutory retention periods and chain-of-custody rules, so switching vendors means migrating material that may be subpoenaed. Add public-agency procurement inertia and multi-year contracts, and the practical switching cost is far above the sticker price of the software. The result is a net revenue retention rate of 126%.

Axon's moat is not the TASER patent estate and it is not the camera hardware, both of which are replicable. It is that the customer's legally sensitive records live inside Axon's cloud, inside Axon's workflow. A police department that wants to switch vendors must migrate an evidence archive subject to statutory retention schedules and chain-of-custody requirements, re-train officers on new capture and tagging procedures, and re-certify the whole process with prosecutors who have to be able to authenticate the material in court. That is not a software migration; it is a legal exercise. Layer on a customer type that runs multi-year competitive procurements and rarely re-bids a working system mid-term, and the practical churn rate collapses. The numbers confirm it: 126% net revenue retention, $15.1B of future contracted revenue growing 41%, and a contract tail that runs roughly ten years past the next twelve months. The moat is measurable, and it is widening — Platform Solutions grew 123% by selling more products into accounts Axon already owns.

Customer concentration in law-enforcement budgets. 82% of Q2 2026 revenue ($742.3M of $904.4M) came from the United States, overwhelmingly from state and local public safety agencies. Those budgets are set by elected bodies and move with public sentiment about policing. A durable political shift toward defunding, or a wave of municipal fiscal stress, hits the whole revenue base at once — this is not diversified end-market risk.

Valuation as its own vulnerability. At a 236× trailing P/E, the stock carries no margin for a growth deceleration. The stock is already −27.41% over the trailing 52 weeks despite 35% revenue growth and raised guidance, which tells you the multiple, not the business, is doing the moving. See Section 04.

Stock-based compensation and GAAP dilution. FY2026 SBC is guided to $590–620M, of which roughly $280M relates to the Employee XSP and the CEO Performance Award. Against Q2 GAAP net income of $29.4M, SBC of $144.0M in the same quarter is roughly five times reported profit. It is the primary reason GAAP earnings look nothing like the adjusted figures analysts model.

Cash conversion has become a live question. Management withdrew its operating cash flow and free cash flow guidance, which had previously pointed to $600M and $450M respectively. Trailing-twelve-month FCF is $133.19M against $265.81M of operating cash flow, with FY2026 capex guided at $160–190M. For a business valued on long-dated contracted revenue, the rate at which that revenue turns into cash is the number to watch.

Notable One-Off Items

Stock-based compensation is the single largest distortion, and it is only partly one-off. Q2 2026 SBC was $144.0M; FY2025 SBC was $634.2M; FY2026 is guided to $590–620M. Of that, roughly $280M relates to the Employee XSP program and the CEO Performance Award — a multi-tranche, milestone-gated equity grant to founder-CEO Rick Smith that vests only on hitting escalating market-capitalisation and operational targets. The award is defensible on incentive grounds and it has, so far, been earned. But it has two effects investors should price separately: it suppresses GAAP earnings (Q2 GAAP net income $29.4M / $0.36 EPS vs. non-GAAP $155.5M / $1.88), and it dilutes share count. When an analyst quotes a "60× forward P/E," they are using the non-GAAP number that adds this back. On GAAP the multiple is 236×.

Guidance withdrawal. Axon pulled its FY2026 operating cash flow and free cash flow guidance, previously $600M and $450M. No replacement figure was given. This is a genuine one-off disclosure event and a reason to weight the contracted-revenue metrics over the earnings metrics.

Acquisition contribution. Dedrone (counter-drone) and Fusus (real-time operations) are acquired businesses now embedded in the reported lines — Dedrone has passed $100M of revenue, and Fusus is cited as a driver of Software & Services growth. Platform Solutions' +123% is therefore part organic, part acquired; Axon does not split the two in the headline release.

Also note: GAAP net income ($199.30M TTM) exceeds GAAP operating income (1.73% × $3.22B ≈ $55.7M), meaning below-the-line items — interest income on the cash pile and investment marks — are contributing more than the operating business on a GAAP basis. Treat the trailing P/E denominator with that in mind.

What the multiple actually implies

Stating it numerically, as promised, and not softening it. At $566.56, market capitalisation is ~$46.0B (81.24M shares × close) and enterprise value is ~$47.2B ($46.0B + $1.85B debt − $0.69B cash). Against $3.22B TTM revenue that is 14.7× sales. Against annualised Q2 adjusted EBITDA ($242M × 4 = $968M) it is ~48.7× EV/adjusted EBITDA; on the GAAP basis the data provider reports 285.78× EV/EBITDA.

Run the arithmetic forward. Q2 non-GAAP net income annualises to roughly $622M, so the stock trades at about 74× annualised non-GAAP earnings. For that to compress to a mature-compounder multiple of 25× without the share price falling, earnings must roughly triple. Holding the 25.5% adjusted EBITDA margin flat, tripling earnings means tripling revenue. Tripling revenue takes 4.2 years at 30% annual growth, 4.9 years at 25%, and 6.0 years at 20%. So the price is underwriting something close to five more years of 25–30% compounding with no margin degradation and no multiple overshoot on the way down. That is not impossible — the company has now done 30%+ for ten straight quarters and has $15.1B of contracted revenue behind it — but it is the bet, stated plainly. 08 Summary & Key Risks

Bull Case

A near-monopoly position in a budget-funded market with ten-year contract tails, and the numbers prove the lock-in rather than assert it: 126% net revenue retention, $15.1B of future contracted revenue growing 41%, ARR of $1.639B growing 39%, and ten consecutive quarters of 30%+ revenue growth. Product attach is compounding — Platform Solutions grew 123% and Axon just signed its first full-scope Axon 911 customer, pushing the company from the evidence archive into the dispatch layer. Software gross margin of 71.3% (over 80% software-only) against 51.9% hardware means every point of mix shift is a point of company margin. International and enterprise bookings roughly tripled off a small base. If growth holds at 25–30% for another four to five years, today's price is defensible.

Bear Case

Almost entirely valuation, and it should not be softened. 236× trailing GAAP earnings, 73.5× forward consensus EPS, 14.7× sales, ~48.7× EV/adjusted EBITDA — among the highest multiples in the S&P 500. Tripling earnings to reach a 25× multiple takes 4.2 years at 30% growth and 6.0 years at 20%; the price underwrites roughly five years of flawless compounding. The market has already begun voting: −27.41% over 52 weeks despite a beat-and-raise. Underneath that, 82% of revenue depends on U.S. law-enforcement budgets set by elected bodies and subject to public sentiment about policing. GAAP economics are thin — 1.73% operating margin, 1.18% ROIC — because $590–620M of annual stock-based compensation, including ~$280M for the Employee XSP and CEO Performance Award, consumes nearly all operating profit and dilutes holders. And cash flow guidance was withdrawn.

01
Multiple compression, not business deterioration, is the dominant risk. At 236× trailing earnings the stock needs perfection. A single quarter of sub-30% growth, or a decline in net revenue retention below 120%, would likely reprice the shares materially even with the backlog intact — the stock already fell on a 35% growth quarter with raised guidance.

Public-safety budgets are the whole end market
CRITICAL CONSTRAINT
$742.3M of Q2 2026's $904.4M — 82% — came from the United States, dominated by state and local law enforcement. That is not a diversified customer base; it is one funding mechanism, appropriated annually by city councils and state legislatures, responsive to public opinion about policing. The offsetting fact is duration: $15.1B of future contracted revenue, with only 20–25% converting in the next twelve months and the balance over roughly ten years. Long contracts do not make the end market safe, but they make it slow — a sentiment shift takes years to reach the income statement.
AI is turning the archive into a product
EMERGING TAILWIND
Axon reported AI Era revenue growing nearly 700% year over year, with the AI Era Plan named as a primary driver of Software & Services' 36% growth. The strategic logic is tight: AI-assisted report writing and video search are only valuable if you already hold the footage, and Axon holds it. That converts a storage cost centre into a premium upsell — and it is visible in the 126% net revenue retention. The cost side shows up too: Software & Services gross margin fell from 75.6% to 71.3% year over year, partly on the compute those features consume.
