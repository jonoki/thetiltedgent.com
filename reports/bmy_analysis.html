01 Company Overview

Bristol-Myers Squibb discovers, develops, manufactures and sells biopharmaceuticals across oncology, hematology, immunology, cardiovascular and — since the Karuna acquisition — neuroscience. Fiscal 2025 revenue was $48.19B, essentially flat year on year (−0.2% reported, −1% excluding currency), with TTM revenue through Q2 2026 of $49.19B (+3.1% YoY). It is the #170 most valuable listed company globally and roughly #119 in the US by market capitalisation, and it sits inside a 21-company "Drug Manufacturers — General" peer group with $4.11T of combined market value and $783B of combined revenue — of which BMY is about 6.3% of revenue but only 3.3% of market value.

That gap is the entire story. Management now reports the business in two buckets: a Growth Portfolio (Opdivo and the subcutaneous Opdivo Qvantig, Reblozyl, Breyanzi, Camzyos, Sotyktu, Krazati, Opdualag, Zeposia, Abecma and Cobenfy) that generated $26.41B in FY2025, +17% YoY, and a Legacy Portfolio (Eliquis, Revlimid, Pomalyst, Sprycel and older brands) that generated $21.79B, −15% YoY. In Q2 2026 the split widened further: Growth $7.56B (+15%) versus Legacy $5.42B (−4%). The market is pricing BMY at 14.8× P/E and ~9.8× the midpoint of FY2026 non-GAAP EPS guidance because it does not believe the Growth Portfolio can outrun what is coming for Eliquis and Opdivo.

03 Competitive Moat & Vulnerabilities
SOURCES OF MOAT
PATENT ESTATE IO FRANCHISE SCALE CELL-THERAPY MANUFACTURING 71.7% GROSS MARGIN $11.4B FREE CASH FLOW GLOBAL REGULATORY REACH SUBCUTANEOUS REFORMULATION

BMS's moat is the ordinary pharmaceutical moat — time-limited monopolies granted by patent, defended by regulatory barriers to entry and funded by extraordinary unit economics — and the numbers confirm how powerful that is while it lasts. Gross margin is 71.66%, operating margin 31.79%, ROE 46.60% and ROIC 21.68%, on $11.44B of free cash flow. Two elements are more durable than the patent estate itself. The first is manufacturing: Breyanzi and Abecma are autologous cell therapies where the product is made from each individual patient's own cells, an operational moat that generic and biosimilar entrants cannot simply copy off a molecule sheet. The second is reformulation: Opdivo Qvantig converts intravenous Opdivo patients to a subcutaneous injection with its own, later exclusivity clock — $261M in Q2 2026 growing more than 200%, annualising above $1B. Every patient converted before IV loss of exclusivity is revenue that does not evaporate on the cliff date. That is the moat BMS is actively manufacturing rather than merely defending.

KEY VULNERABILITIES

1. Concentration in two expiring assets. Eliquis ($14,443M FY2025) and Opdivo IV ($10,049M FY2025) together are 50.8% of company revenue and both lose exclusivity this decade. No large-cap pharmaceutical company carries a comparable single-decade exposure. The Growth Portfolio grew 17% in FY2025 off a $26.41B base — roughly $4.5B of incremental revenue a year. Replacing $24.5B therefore takes five-plus years of uninterrupted 15–17% compounding, and that assumes nothing in the Growth Portfolio itself stumbles.

2. US drug pricing policy is now a dated, quantified headwind, not a risk. Per the Q2 2026 10-Q: Eliquis became subject to the Inflation Reduction Act's maximum fair price on January 1, 2026; Pomalyst follows on January 1, 2027; Orencia was selected for negotiation beginning 2028. That is three of BMS's largest products under administered pricing inside three years. Proposed most-favoured-nation pricing and pharmaceutical tariffs sit on top of that as unquantified additional exposure.

3. The business-development machine has been expensive and, so far, unproductive. BMS expensed $13.373B of acquired IPR&D in 2024 and $3.721B in 2025. The flagship asset from that spending, Cobenfy, is annualising around $250M. The newest deal — a global collaboration with Hengrui covering 13 early-stage assets, $600M upfront in Q3 2026 and up to $14.3B in milestones — is early-stage by construction, meaning it cannot contribute revenue before the cliff arrives.

4. Leverage constrains the response. Total debt is $45.05B against $11.07B of cash — roughly $34B net debt, about 1.8× EBITDA implied by the $171.02B enterprise value and 9.03× EV/EBITDA. Debt-to-equity is 2.02. A company that needs to buy its way out of a patent cliff has less balance-sheet room to do so than the peers it is bidding against, and a 3.75% dividend it is unlikely to cut.

NOTABLE ONE-OFF ITEMS

Acquired IPR&D is the dominant distortion, and it is enormous. BMS expensed $13.373B of acquired in-process R&D in FY2024 — principally the Karuna transaction — which converted an otherwise profitable year into a GAAP net loss of $8,948M and GAAP EPS of −$4.41. FY2025 acquired IPR&D was $3.721B (led by the Orbital Therapeutics acquisition), leaving GAAP EPS of $3.46 against non-GAAP EPS of $6.15. In the first half of 2026 the run-rate collapsed to $94M versus $1,695M in the first half of 2025 — which is the single largest reason the trailing GAAP P/E of 14.81 looks so much better than it did a year ago. Anyone comparing BMY's GAAP earnings across years is comparing noise. Use non-GAAP EPS or free cash flow.

IPR&D impairments. Separate from acquired IPR&D expense, the Q2 2026 release shows $420M of IPR&D impairments in the quarter and $830M year-to-date — writedowns of programmes that failed. These recur often enough at BMS that treating them as strictly non-recurring is generous.

Restructuring. The 2023 restructuring plan carries ~$2.5B of total expected charges, of which $1.8B has been incurred to date, targeting ~$2.0B of annual cost savings by the end of 2027. Management describes this as a company-wide strategic productivity initiative running through R&D and SG&A. FY2026 operating expense guidance is ~$16.5B.

Medicare Part D redesign / IRA effects. Eliquis has been subject to maximum fair pricing since January 1, 2026 — yet Eliquis grew 22% in Q2 2026, which strongly suggests gross-to-net and Part D redesign mechanics are running in BMS's favour in the short term and will not persist. Pomalyst's maximum fair price starts January 1, 2027 and Orencia enters negotiation for 2028. Treat 2026 Eliquis growth as a timing artefact, not a trend.

Business development. The Hengrui collaboration signed for the third quarter of 2026 carries a $600M upfront payment booked in Q3 2026 against up to $14.3B of potential milestones across 13 early-stage assets. Expect the upfront to hit Q3 GAAP earnings.

08 Summary & Key Risks

Bull Case

The Growth Portfolio has already crossed over: $26.41B in FY2025 growing 17%, and $7.56B (+15%) in Q2 2026 — nearly 58% of revenue. Camzyos +60%, Breyanzi +41%, Reblozyl +29% and Opdivo Qvantig +200%-plus are real, compounding franchises with long exclusivity runways, and Qvantig specifically defuses part of the Opdivo cliff by converting patients to a later-expiring formulation. Meanwhile the stock trades at 14.8× trailing and ~9.8× guided non-GAAP earnings against a 31.79× peer group, throws off $11.44B of free cash flow (8.3% yield) and pays 3.75% covered roughly twice over. Management raised FY2026 guidance by ~$3B of revenue and $0.70 of EPS in July. If the cliff is merely severe rather than catastrophic, the multiple has room to double.

Bear Case

Eliquis ($14.4B) plus Opdivo IV ($10.0B) is 50.8% of revenue facing loss of exclusivity this decade, and the Growth Portfolio adds only about $4.5B a year — five-plus years of flawless compounding just to stand still. Consensus already models revenue falling 3.4% and EPS falling 5.6% in 2027, before the worst of it. The $13.4B of 2024 acquired IPR&D bought Cobenfy, which annualises at roughly $250M, and its Alzheimer's-psychosis readout has slipped to 2027; Milvexian's atrial fibrillation readout slipped to Q1 2027. Eliquis is under IRA maximum fair pricing from January 2026, Pomalyst from 2027, Orencia negotiated from 2028. With $45.05B of debt and 2.02× debt-to-equity, the balance sheet cannot fund a rescue acquisition of the required size. A low multiple on a shrinking base is not value — it is arithmetic.

TOP 3 RISKS
01
The Eliquis cliff is unhedged and it is 30% of the company. Eliquis generated $14,443M in FY2025 and grew 22% in Q2 2026 — growth that is almost certainly a Part D redesign and gross-to-net timing artefact rather than demand, since maximum fair pricing took effect January 1, 2026. The designated successor, milvexian, does not read out in atrial fibrillation until Q1 2027, and if that trial disappoints there is no second shot before exclusivity lapses.
02
Cobenfy is not scaling fast enough to justify what was paid for it. FY2025 revenue of $155M and a Q2 2026 run-rate near $250M sit against $13.373B of acquired IPR&D expensed in 2024. Management's remedy — prescriber education on dose optimisation — addresses tolerability and persistence, which is an admission that the launch curve is shallower than modelled. The Alzheimer's disease psychosis readout that would change the economics has slipped to 2027 on enrolment problems.
03
Leverage plus policy leaves no room for error. $45.05B of debt, 2.02× debt-to-equity and a 3.75% dividend that management will be extremely reluctant to cut, against three of the largest products entering administered US pricing between 2026 and 2028 and unquantified most-favoured-nation and tariff exposure on top. The Hengrui deal ($600M upfront, up to $14.3B in milestones, 13 early-stage assets) is the right strategic direction but is far too early-stage to bridge 2028–2030.
  Industry Commentary
The patent cliff arithmetic facing big pharma
CRITICAL CONSTRAINT
The whole large-cap pharmaceutical sector is walking into a late-decade exclusivity wall, but BMY's version is the most concentrated. Its two largest products — Eliquis at $14,443M and Opdivo at $10,049M in FY2025 — are 50.8% of revenue and both expire this decade. The offsetting engine is real but under-sized: the Growth Portfolio's FY2025 +17% on a $26.41B base is roughly $4.5B of incremental revenue a year, while the Legacy Portfolio is shedding roughly $3.3B a year at its FY2025 −15% rate. Net-net that produces the flat revenue actually observed (FY2025 −0.2%, TTM +3.1%). The problem is that today's Legacy decline is being cushioned by an Eliquis that is still growing; when Eliquis rolls over, the decline rate steps up sharply while the Growth Portfolio's absolute contribution grows only linearly. Consensus already sees this: −3.4% revenue and −5.6% EPS in 2027.
US drug pricing policy and tariffs
ONGOING HEADWIND
The Inflation Reduction Act has moved from theoretical to dated and specific for BMS. Per the Q2 2026 10-Q: Eliquis under maximum fair pricing since January 1, 2026; Pomalyst from January 1, 2027; Orencia ($3,705M FY2025) selected for negotiation beginning 2028. That is roughly $20B of FY2025 revenue moving under administered US pricing inside three years. Layered on top are most-favoured-nation reference-pricing proposals, which would compress the US price premium that cross-subsidises global R&D, and pharmaceutical tariffs, which would hit deliberately globalised manufacturing networks. Neither is quantifiable from disclosure — and unquantifiable policy risk is precisely what keeps a 71.7% gross-margin business trading at 14.8× earnings while the peer group trades at 31.79×.
Does a low multiple compensate for a shrinking base?
THE CENTRAL QUESTION
Bull framing: at 14.8× trailing and ~9.8× guided non-GAAP EPS, with an 8.3% free-cash-flow yield and a 3.75% dividend covered roughly twice, the market is discounting a permanent decline. It only takes the Growth Portfolio holding mid-teens growth — which it did at +17% in FY2025 and +15% in Q2 2026 — plus one successful large label expansion (Cobenfy in Alzheimer's psychosis) or one clean milvexian readout for the terminal-decline assumption to break. Beta of 0.22 means you are paid to wait with very little market risk. Bear framing: low multiples on assets with contractual expiry dates are not mean-reverting — they are correct. Buying a 10× forward multiple on 2026 earnings is worthless if 2029 earnings are half of 2026's, and the two catalysts that could change the trajectory both slipped a year in the same quarter. The honest resolution: this is a timing and probability bet on 2027 readouts and business development, not a valuation bet, and it should be sized as such.
