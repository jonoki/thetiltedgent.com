01 Overview
In brief — why it matters
  • What it is used for today. BNB pays the fees on BNB Smart Chain (BSC): 18,080,802 transactions on September 22, 2026 (BscScan). Applications on BSC held $5.91B and the chain carried $16.90B of dollar stablecoins (DefiLlama). Binance, the exchange that created BNB, gives a 25% trading-fee discount for paying in BNB, "valid until further notice" (Binance announcement, as archived August 1, 2026).
  • How adoption grew. Sold in a 2017 token sale to fund Binance; fee token of Binance's own chain from April 2019 and of BSC from September 2020. Fourth by market value ($104.9B) after bitcoin, ether and tether in CoinGecko's 00:00 UTC September 23, 2026 snapshot (the September 22 close).
  • What moves the price. No new BNB is created; the last four quarterly burns removed 6.0M BNB (4.5%). Filed fund and company holdings are under 0.5% of supply. BNB fell 9–11% on the days of Binance's 2023 US legal events. Beta to the S&P 500 1.68, correlation 0.47; correlation with bitcoin 0.72 (60 months, our calculation).
  • Competition. Other smart-contract chains: on September 22, 2026 Ethereum held 56.5% of DefiLlama's tracked value (BSC 6.1%), and Tron carried $94.02B of dollar stablecoins (BSC $16.90B).
  • The open question. How much of BNB's demand still rests on one company, Binance, whose 2023 US settlements exceeded $4.3B.

What it is. BNB is "the native coin of the BNB Chain ecosystem". It pays every transaction fee on BNB Smart Chain and is staked to elect the chain's validators; "Holding BNB grants the right to participate in BNB Chain's decentralized on-chain governance" (BNB Chain blog, June 2024).

How it was created. BNB began as a start-up exchange's token. Its founding document, the Binance Exchange Whitepaper V1.1 (June 2017), says: "We will issue our token coin, called the Binance Coin. A strict limit of 200MM BNB will be created, never to be increased. BNB will run natively on the Ethereum blockchain with ERC 20." Half was sold in a June 26 – July 3, 2017 token sale, 40% went to the founding team and 10% to angel investors. BNB later moved to Binance's own chains: Binance Chain (April 2019) and BNB Smart Chain (BSC, September 1, 2020).

The stated purpose. A fee token first: "You can use BNB to pay for any fees on our platform", at a discount that started at 50%, with a chain role planned from the start (see below). The burn was planned too: "Every quarter, we will use 20% of our profits to buy back BNB and destroy them, until we buy 50% of all the BNB (100MM) back."

Consensus: proof of staked authority. A limited set of validators, elected by stake, produce blocks: on September 22, 2026, 45 of 56 registered validators, with 25.05M BNB staked. Blocks come every 0.45 seconds, down from 3 seconds before 2025.

Supply: fixed at creation, shrinking by burns. Validators are paid from fees, not new coins. Since December 2021 the quarterly burn follows a published formula, Auto-Burn, instead of Binance's profits: it burns more when the price is lower and more blocks are produced, until supply falls below 100 million. Under BEP-95, 10% of every block's gas fee is also burned.

Token sale, burn formula, burn history and supply method

Token sale and launch detail: the whitepaper is dated by its own schedule, which lists June 22, 2017 for its release; BNB Chain dates the sale "June 26th to July 3rd, 2017, just before the Binance Exchange opened for trading" (June 6, 2024). The whitepaper also planned the chain role: "In the future, Binance will build a decentralized exchange, where BNB will be used as one of the key base assets as well as gas to be spent." Allocation 100,000,000 BNB to the sale, 80,000,000 to the founding team (20% released at the start, then 20% a year for four years) and 20,000,000 to angel investors; planned sale price 2,700 BNB per ETH in week one, falling to 2,300. The SEC's 2023 complaint puts the sale at about $15 million, "selling 100 million BNB tokens at an average price of $0.15 per token." Fee discount schedule: 50%, 25%, 12.5%, 6.75%, then none in year five. Binance Chain (later Beacon Chain) launched April 18, 2019; BSC's block 1 is timestamped August 29, 2020, 03:24:09 UTC, and BNB Chain announced its mainnet on September 1, 2020. Block-time cuts (191,937 blocks on September 22, 2026, one every 0.45 s; BscScan): Lorentz (April 29, 2025, 1.5 s), Maxwell (June 30, 2025, 0.75 s), Fermi (January 14, 2026, 0.45 s). Auto-Burn formula (Binance, December 2021): B = N × 1000 / (P + K), where "N is the total number of blocks produced on BSC during the calendar quarter. P is an average price of BNB against the US dollar. K is a constant value as a price anchor, initially set at 1000." BEP-520 restates it in time rather than blocks, B = (T/3) × 1000 / (P + K) with T the seconds in the quarter, and the burn posts since July 2025 say that after the block-time upgrades "The parameters used in the formula have been adjusted to keep the idea and spirit consistent."

Quarterly Auto-Burns (BNB Chain blog, stated figures): 32nd, Jul 10, 2025: 1,595,599.78 BNB, remaining supply 139,289,513.94 · 33rd, Oct 27, 2025: 1,441,281.413 BNB, 137,738,379.26 · 34th, Jan 15, 2026: 1,371,803.77 BNB, 136,361,374.34 · 35th, Apr 15, 2026: 1,569,307.34 BNB, 134,786,916.53 · 36th, Jul 15, 2026: 1,615,827.795 BNB (~$931.7M at the time), 133,166,127.91 "at time of writing 15 July, 2026 at 10:35AM UTC". The 36th was sent to the address 0x…dEaD on BSC.

Real-time burn (BEP-95): "A fixed ratio of the gas fee collected by the validators will be burned in each block"; initial ratio 10%. The ratio read from the ValidatorSet contract at the first block of September 22, 2026 was still 10%. BNB Chain's burn posts put the cumulative BEP-95 burn at "roughly 291K BNB" (July 2026), against 265K a year earlier.

Supply used on this page: 133,166,127.91 BNB, the supply stated after the 36th burn; the real-time burn since then is not deducted. The stated supply fell 4.40% between the 32nd and 36th burn posts (139,289,513.94 → 133,166,127.91). CoinGecko's market value at 00:00 UTC on September 23, 2026 was $104.95B, against our $104.96B.

Consensus (BSC whitepaper): "Blocks are produced by a limited set of validators"; the validator set is elected "based on a staking-based governance."

Staking: sum of totalPooledBNB() across the 56 StakeCredit contracts registered in StakeHub, at block 123,273,382 (00:00:00 UTC, Sep 22, 2026): 25,048,037.36 BNB. The contract source describes this as "total reward plus total BNB staked in the pool".

03 Network Strengths & Vulnerabilities

Strengths:

18.1M transactions a day 0.45-second blocks No new issuance; 4.5% burned in a year $16.9B of dollar stablecoins 25.05M BNB staked

BSC's strength is cheap, fast block space linked to Binance, the exchange that created it: BscScan's average transaction fee on September 22 was $0.04.

Key Vulnerabilities

01
A small validator set, one main client. 45 elected validators produce blocks, and bnb-chain/bsc is the one main client; in October 2022 the chain was halted "by contacting community validators one by one".
02
Dependence on Binance. Binance created BNB; BNB fell 9–11% on the days of Binance's 2023 US legal events (section 07).
03
Bridge and application risk. The October 2022 bridge exploit withdrew "a total of 2 million BNB" (BNB Chain).

Self-custody, exchange custody and staking. Holding BNB directly means controlling the private keys: no one can freeze the coins, but a lost key cannot be recovered. An ETF holds coins with a named custodian and charges a yearly fee.

08 Summary & Key Risks

BNB is an exchange token that became a chain's fuel. Its price rests on fee demand on BSC, a supply that only shrinks, and Binance's fortunes. Binance settled with US agencies in 2023, the SEC case was dismissed in 2025 and the founder was pardoned in 2025. What would change the picture:

Bull Case

BSC's applications and stablecoins growing faster than rival chains', lifting fee demand; the planned doubling of throughput landing; fund and company holdings growing from under 0.5% of supply, with one US spot BNB ETF trading since May 28, 2026 and more filed. Each of the last four burns removed over 1% of supply.

Bear Case

BSC losing applications and stablecoins to Ethereum, Solana or Tron; new enforcement against Binance, a loss of its users, or a change to the BNB fee discount; an outage in a 45-validator, one-client network. BNB is not among the SEC's 2026 examples of digital commodities.
