01 Overview

A Government of Canada bond is a loan to the federal government in Canadian dollars. The Bank of Canada's daily 5-year benchmark yield is the mid-market closing yield of one selected issue: since September 10, 2026, the 3.00% bond due September 1, 2031. At 3.60% on October 2, 2026, it is 0.88 pp above a year earlier (2.72%) and 0.27 pp above end-August.

Auction and coupon mechanics

A new bond's coupon is "set to the nearest one-quarter of one percent below the average yield of the accepted competitive bids": the benchmark's first auction averaged 3.088%, so its coupon is 3.00%. At reopenings winners pay "the price equivalents of the accepted bid yields." Eight $5.0B auctions (April 8 – September 9, 2026) took it to $40.0B. The Bank changes its 5-year benchmark "typically after the last auction for that bond"; the next 5-year auctions sell a new bond due March 1, 2032.

Credit. Payments are "a direct charge on, and payable out of, the Consolidated Revenue Fund of Canada" (Standard Terms). Key Vulnerabilities

01
Rate risk. A 1-point rise in yield cuts the price about 4.4%, close to a year and a half of coupons.
02
Inflation. The coupon is fixed; CPI rose 3.0% in the year to August.
03
Reinvestment. Coupons reinvested below 3.60% lower the realised return.
04
Supply. Four 5-year auctions of the new March 2032 bond are scheduled between October 15 and December 2.

08 Summary & Key Risks

When it does well

When yields fall: about +4.6% per 1-point drop, plus the coupon. That tends to follow easing inflation, weaker growth or expected rate cuts. Held to September 2031, it repays face value whatever the price does.

When it does poorly

When yields rise: about −4.4% per point, close to a year and a half of coupons. That tends to follow inflation staying above target, an expected rise in the policy rate, or rising global yields.

Top 3 Risks

01
Higher yields. −12.5% at +3 points on full repricing.
02
Sticky inflation. The Bank: CPI "has been hovering around 3% in recent months."
03
Global spillover. U.S. 5-year par yield: 5.06% on Oct 2, 2026.
