01 Overview

A 30-year Treasury Inflation-Protected Security (TIPS) is a 30-year loan to the U.S. government whose principal moves with consumer prices. TreasuryDirect: "Unlike other Treasury securities, where the principal is fixed, the principal of a TIPS can go up or down over its term." The index is the CPI-U. The fixed coupon is paid on the adjusted principal, and at maturity: "you get either the increased (inflation-adjusted) price or the original principal, whichever is greater. You never get less than the original principal."

The header, 3.34%, is Treasury's 30-year par real yield for October 2, 2026, the highest in Treasury's daily 30-year real series, which starts on February 22, 2010. With the nominal 30-year at 5.63%, 30-year breakeven inflation was 2.29%.

Auction, coupon and index ratio

In a single-price auction every winner pays the price set by the highest accepted real yield. The coupon is set at the first auction ("never less than 0.125%"); the August reopening sold more of the same bond. Price paid = real price × index ratio. On October 2 the ratio was 1.03044 (333.95226 ÷ 324.08800), so $1,000 of principal stood at $1,030.44.

03 Strengths & Vulnerabilities
Direct U.S. Treasury obligation Moody's Aa1 (stable) Principal indexed to CPI-U Par floor at maturity No state or local income tax

Credit. Moody's: "Moody's Ratings downgrades United States ratings to Aa1 from Aaa; changes outlook to stable" (May 16, 2025). S&P and Fitch: n/v. Demand. The August 20 reopening drew $22.56B of bids for $8.0B, excluding the Fed's $1.03B add-on; February's new issue $24.74B for $9.0B.

Key Vulnerabilities

01
Real-rate risk. A 1-point rise in the real yield cuts the price about 17.4% (duration 19.91).
02
Low or falling CPI. Principal falls with deflation; the par floor applies only at maturity, in 2056.
03
Tax on principal growth. Principal increases can be taxed in the year they occur; the cash comes at maturity or sale.
04
Thin issue. Two sales a year: $17.0B of 30-year TIPS offered in 2026, against $99B of 10-year TIPS (five sales to September).

08 Summary & Key Risks

When it does well

When real yields fall (about +22.5% for a 1-point drop), and when CPI-U inflation runs above the 2.29% breakeven: principal and coupons rise with the index while a nominal bond's stay fixed.

When it does poorly

When real yields rise (about −17.4% for a 1-point rise), and when inflation runs below the breakeven or CPI falls: principal is marked down, and the par floor applies only at maturity.

Top 3 Risks

01
Further rises in real yields. −42.0% at +3 pp (full repricing).
02
Inflation below the 2.29% breakeven. The nominal 30-year bond then earns more.
03
Tax before cash. Principal increases can be taxed yearly; the cash comes later.
