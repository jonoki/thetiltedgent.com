01 Overview

A Treasury bond is a loan to the U.S. federal government, sold with a 20- or 30-year term. The 20-year is the younger: on May 20, 2020 Treasury sold one for the first time since 1986. That bond pays 1.125% and matures May 15, 2040.

The header is Treasury's 20-year par yield for October 2, 2026: 5.67%, above both the 10-year (5.28%) and the 30-year (5.63%). Only one close since July 5, 2002 was higher: September 30, 2026 (5.68%), the highest since June 12, 2002.

More: sources for the history

Treasury announced the 20-year's return on January 16, 2020 and set its terms in the February 5, 2020 refunding statement; the May 20, 2020 sale was the first since 1986 (Federal Reserve Bank of New York). The 2002 comparison uses Treasury's daily files, which carry a 20-year rate from October 1993.

Auctions and the current issue

Key Vulnerabilities

01
Rate risk. Modified duration 11.99: a 1-point rise cuts the price about 11.0%, a little over two years of coupons.
02
Inflation risk. Fixed dollar coupons for 20 years. Inflation above the 2.48% 20-year breakeven leaves it behind a 20-year TIPS.
03
Reinvestment risk. The 5.67% yield assumes every coupon is reinvested at 5.67%.
04
Fiscal and supply risk. A 20-year auction every month of 2026 so far, $13–16B each.

More: the formulas behind these figures

Formulas (semi-annual, actual/actual): y = 0.0567; c = 2.5625 per 100 per half-year; settlement Oct 5, 2026 (T+1), 51 of 184 days into the coupon period; first cash flow in w = 133/184 half-years, n = 40 flows (last 102.5625). tk = w + k; PVk = CFk / (1 + y/2)tk; Pfull = ΣPVk = 94.26. Macaulay D = Σ tk·PVk / P / 2 = 12.33 years. Convexity C = Σ PVk·tk(tk+1) / [P·(1 + y/2)²·4] = 197.7. Clean = 94.26 − 2.5625 × 51/184 = 93.55.

08 Summary & Key Risks

When it does well

When long yields fall: −1 pp lifts the price about 13.0%, plus the 5.125% coupon. Drivers: inflation below the 2.48% breakeven, a lower policy path, a flight to safety. Held to 2046, it repays face value.

When it does poorly

When long yields rise: +1 pp cuts the price about 11.0%. Drivers: inflation surprises, a higher policy path, heavier long-bond supply.

Top 3 Risks

01
Further rises in long yields. −11.1% at +1 pp, −28.5% at +3 pp (full repricing).
02
Inflation above 2.48% for two decades. The Fed's 2026 PCE median is 3.7%.
03
Fiscal credit and supply. Aa1 since May 2025; $13–16B auctioned every month.
Rates Commentary
The year's rise is almost all real yield
DECOMPOSITION

5.67% = 3.19% real yield (20-year TIPS) + 2.48% breakeven. A year earlier: 2.22% + 2.44%. Of the 1.01-point rise, 0.97 is real yield.

A bump in the curve
CURVE

At end-May 2020, just after its return, the 20-year (1.18%) sat between the 10-year (0.65%) and 30-year (1.41%). Since 2021 it has mostly yielded more than the 30-year (Section 02).
