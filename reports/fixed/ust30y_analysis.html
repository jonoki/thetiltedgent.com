Key Vulnerabilities

01
Rate risk. Modified duration 14.85: a 1-point rise cuts the price about 13.2%, some 2.6 years of coupons.
02
Inflation risk. Fixed dollar coupons for 30 years. Inflation above the 2.22% breakeven leaves it behind the 30-year TIPS.
03
Reinvestment risk. The 5.28% yield assumes coupons are reinvested at 5.28%.
04
Fiscal and supply risk. A 30-year auction every month of 2026 so far, $22–25B each.

08 Summary & Key Risks

When it does well

When long yields fall: −1 pp lifts the price about 16.5%, plus the 5.125% coupon. Drivers: inflation below the 2.22% breakeven, a lower policy path, a flight to safety. Held to 2056, it pays face value whatever the price does.

When it does poorly

When long yields rise: +1 pp cuts the price about 13.2%. Drivers: inflation surprises (2026 PCE median 3.7%), a higher policy path (2026 fed funds median 3.8 → 4.1), heavier supply.

Top 3 Risks

01
Further rises in long yields. −13.3% at +1 pp, −32.8% at +3 pp (full repricing).
02
Inflation above 2.22% for decades. The Fed's 2026 PCE median is 3.7%.
03
Fiscal credit and supply. Aa1 since May 2025; $22–25B auctioned monthly.
Rates Commentary
The rise is all real yield
DECOMPOSITION

5.28% = 3.06% real yield (30-year TIPS) + 2.22% breakeven. A year earlier: 2.44% + 2.23%. Breakeven barely moved, so the 0.61-point rise is all real yield (+0.62).

The long end moved least
CURVE

The 30-year minus 2-year gap fell from 1.07 to 0.69 points over the year. The September SEP raised every projected policy rate shared with June.
