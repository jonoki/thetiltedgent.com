01 Overview

A Treasury bill is a short loan to the U.S. federal government with no interest payments. TreasuryDirect: "Bills are sold at a discount or at par (face value). When the bill matures, you are paid its face value." The 26-week bill is the curve's 6-month point: at the September 28 auction a buyer paid $978.34 for $1,000 due April 1, 2027.

The header yield, 4.27%, is Treasury's 6-month par yield for October 2, 2026.

Three ways to quote one bill

Bills sell in a single-price auction: every winner pays the price set by the highest accepted discount rate. The discount rate (4.285%) measures the gain against face value on a 360-day year; the investment rate (4.441%) measures it against the price paid on a 365-day year, comparable with coupon yields. The par yield (4.27%) is Treasury's bond-equivalent curve reading for October 2.

Credit quality. Moody's rates the U.S. government Aa1, stable, after downgrading it from Aaa on May 16, 2025. S&P and Fitch: n/v. Liquidity. The September 28 auction drew $224.15B of bids for $82B offered.

Key Vulnerabilities

01
Reinvestment risk. The rate is locked for six months, then the holder gets the next auction's rate.
02
Rate risk. Small: about −0.49% of price per 1-point rise, about twice a 3-month bill's; face value at maturity.
03
Inflation risk. 2026 PCE median 3.7% vs a 4.27% pre-tax yield.
04
Fiscal and supply risk. The 26-week offering rose to $82B on September 28 from $79B at each auction Aug 31–Sep 21.

08 Summary & Key Risks

When it does well

When the policy rate rises or stays high. Each new bill is bought at the latest rate, so income follows rates up within six months.

When it does poorly

When rates fall. A holder rolling bills earns less at each auction (0.05% at end-September 2021), with little duration to gain from falling yields. After inflation and tax the return can be negative.

Top 3 Risks

01
Falling rates on reinvestment. The yield is locked for only 182 days. The SEP longer-run median is 3.2%, below today's 4.27%.
02
Inflation. The Fed's 2026 PCE median is 3.7%, close to the 4.27% bill yield before tax.
03
Fiscal credit and supply. Moody's cut the U.S. to Aa1 in May 2025. Treasury raised the 26-week offering to $82B on September 28; weaker auctions would push bill yields up.
