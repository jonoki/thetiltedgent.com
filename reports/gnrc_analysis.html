01 Company Overview

For most of its life Generac has been a weather stock. It makes the home standby generator that switches on when a storm knocks the grid out, plus portable generators, outdoor power equipment, batteries and smart-home energy devices — and the share price has tended to rise and fall with outage seasons and housing activity. FY2025 was a flat year: revenue of $4.21B, down 2.02%, and diluted EPS of $2.69, down 50.09%.

The 2026 story is a different business growing inside the old one. The Commercial & Industrial (C&I) segment sells large-megawatt backup generators, and hyperscale data centers — which cannot go dark for a second — have become its fastest-growing customer. Q2 2026 C&I sales rose 29% to $556M while Residential slipped 2% to $617M, and the company put its data-center backlog at roughly $1.6B. Then, on September 16, 2026, Generac disclosed a long-term supply agreement with Amazon for data-center backup generators: warrant vesting is tied to up to $8B of Amazon purchases, initial deliveries are expected to total about $2.4B in 2027–2028, and the warrant gives Amazon the right to buy up to 1,693,745 Generac shares at $200.9266 each (per the company's announcement). The next session the stock closed +18.34% on September 17, 2026 at $207.23, having traded as high as $231.89 that day, and settled at $204.96 on September 21. The question for the price is no longer "how many hurricanes" but "how much of a $12B company is now a data-center power supplier, and at what margin".

03 Competitive Moat & Vulnerabilities

Sources of moat (our assessment):

Home-standby brand recognition Installer & dealer network Hyperscaler qualification Multi-year supply contracts Wisconsin manufacturing base Moderate leverage (D/E 0.49)

In residential, the moat is distribution: a standby generator is sold and installed by trained contractors, and in our view a homeowner after a blackout tends to buy whatever brand the local installer carries and can fit soonest. In data centers the moat is being built now. Hyperscalers qualify suppliers through factory visits and performance reviews before signing (Generac's June 2026 announcement, as reported by Rental Equipment Register, cites multiple factory visits, performance and quality-system reviews and audits), and once a generator design is qualified into a data-center template, the switching cost is real. The weakness is that Generac is the newcomer in large-megawatt power, facing incumbents many times its size.

Key Vulnerabilities

01
One customer, a lot of the story. Up to $8B from Amazon against $4.21B of FY2025 total revenue. The warrant ties Amazon's upside to Generac's share price, but the buyer holds the pricing power in a deal this size, and the margin on these units has not been disclosed.
02
Residential is not growing yet. Down 2% in Q2 2026 with energy storage shipments lower. FY2025 revenue fell 2.02% and EPS halved. Full-year guidance needs a strong second half.
03
Product recalls. The U.S. Consumer Product Safety Commission posted a recall of about 149,400 Generac portable generators for fuel-leak fire and burn hazards on April 16, 2026, and an expanded recall of about 16,260 electric-start pressure washers (carbon monoxide hazard from self-starting) on May 14, 2026 (cpsc.gov). The financial impact was not quantified in the sources fetched.
04
Capacity execution. Growth of 30%-plus in C&I requires new plants, acquisitions (Enercon, Belvidere) and hiring. A delivery slip on hyperscaler schedules would hit the one line the market is paying for.

08 Summary & Key Risks

Bull Case

Generac has turned a hurricane-driven consumer business into a supplier to the fastest-growing buyers of power equipment. C&I grew 29% in Q2, the data-center backlog reached ~$1.6B before Amazon, and Amazon adds up to $8B more with ~$2.4B scheduled for 2027–2028. Consensus puts FY2026 EPS at $9.73 and FY2027 at $12.39; on those numbers the stock is about 21x this year and 17x next. Every target on the StockAnalysis panel is above $204.96, and the residential franchise still earns most of the profit while the new business scales.

Bear Case

On what the company has actually earned, the stock is 47x trailing EPS — and that EPS includes a one-off tariff refund. Residential shrank in Q2. The data-center growth depends on a small number of very large buyers who can dictate price, and Amazon's warrant hands part of the upside to the customer. Generac is competing with Caterpillar and Cummins, companies several times its size, for capacity and components. And the stock has already shown what happens when the narrative wobbles: −32.7% in July 2026, and an 11% intraday drop on an Investor Day without a deal.

Top 3 Risks

01
Customer concentration and pricing. A contract worth up to nearly twice FY2025 total revenue gives the buyer leverage on price and terms. Unit margins on hyperscale generators have not been disclosed.
02
Execution on capacity. Growth depends on new plants, the Enercon and Belvidere additions and hiring on schedule. Late deliveries would hit revenue and the relationship.
03
Earnings quality and valuation. 47.1x trailing, with a one-off tariff benefit in the base and forward estimates that assume EPS more than doubles. A 1.91 beta means any miss is expensive.
Industry Commentary
Data centers need power that never stops
EMERGING TAILWIND

Hyperscale data centers typically keep on-site backup generation so a grid failure never reaches the servers. As AI and cloud build-outs add capacity, that demand is flowing to generator makers — and Generac's Q2 release says it is "investing aggressively in incremental production and packaging capacity" to meet it. Generac's C&I segment grew 28% in Q1 and 29% in Q2 2026, and the company has now signed two hyperscale agreements plus Amazon. The durability question is whether this is a multi-year build cycle or a pull-forward — backlog visibility into 2027–2028 is the best evidence so far.

Residential backup power: outages, housing, storage
ONGOING HEADWIND

Home standby demand follows outage events and housing activity. After the 2021 surge, the category normalised and Generac's revenue fell 11.88% in FY2023. In 2026 home standby grew in Q2 while energy storage shipments fell, leaving Residential down 2%. The segment still generated $354M of adjusted EBITDA in the first half — more than twice C&I — so its stability matters to funding the data-center build.

Tariffs cut both ways
WATCH ITEM

Q2 2026 included a ~$71M pre-tax tariff refund that added about 6 points to gross margin. That is a gain from the past, not a run-rate, and trade policy can move input costs for a manufacturer with global supply chains in either direction. Read margin guidance with and without it.
