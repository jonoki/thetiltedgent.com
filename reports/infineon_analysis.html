01 Company Overview

Infineon makes the chips that move and switch electricity — power semiconductors in silicon, silicon carbide and gallium nitride — plus the microcontrollers, sensors and security chips that sit around them. By its own presentation (citing TechInsights and Omdia) it is the world's #1 supplier of automotive chips (12.8% share), power discretes and modules (17.3%) and microcontrollers (23.1%). Fiscal 2025 (to September 30, 2025) revenue was €14.66B with a Segment Result of €2.56B (17.5% margin) — down from €16.37B and 27% in fiscal 2023, the last peak.

The story of 2026 is that the cycle turned and AI arrived at the same time. Every AI server rack needs power conversion from the grid down to the processor, and Infineon now expects more than €1.6B of AI data-center power revenue in fiscal 2026, ahead of a €1.5B plan, plus about €500M of conventional data-center power. Q3 FY2026 (April–June) revenue hit a record €4.172B, +12.6% year on year, and management guides the September quarter to about €4.7B at a ~23% Segment Result margin. The share price ran ahead of all of it: from a €38.00 close on March 31, 2026 to €81.67 on June 30 (+115% in a quarter), then gave back about a fifth to €64.64. At that price the stock trades on 70x trailing IFRS earnings but 23x the street's fiscal-2027 adjusted EPS. The debate is whether fiscal 2027 delivers that jump — consensus has revenue up 20% to €19.66B — or whether, as some brokers now argue, the chip cycle peaks first.

03 Competitive Moat & Vulnerabilities

Sources of moat:

#1 in power semis (17.3%) #1 in automotive chips (12.8%) #1 in microcontrollers (23.1%) Silicon, SiC and GaN in-house Own fabs: Germany, Austria, Malaysia AI capacity reservation deals ~€30B order backlog

The moat is breadth plus capacity. In AI power, Infineon sells the whole chain from the grid connection to the processor's voltage regulator, in all three materials — silicon carbide for high-voltage conversion, gallium nitride for dense intermediate stages, silicon at the chip — and makes them in its own fabs. When supply is short, owning cleanroom space is the advantage: the new Smart Power Fab in Dresden had just opened when Infineon reported on August 5, 2026, and management points to spare cleanroom space at its large front-end sites in Austria and Malaysia. Customers paying prepayments to reserve capacity for several years is the clearest sign that the position is valued. In cars, a microcontroller designed into a platform tends to stay for its life, which is why automotive share moves slowly in either direction.

Key Vulnerabilities

01
The price already assumes a strong 2027. At €64.64 the stock is 70x trailing IFRS EPS of €0.92 and 23x consensus fiscal-2027 adjusted EPS of €2.79 — which itself requires revenue up about 20% and the Segment Result margin rising to 24.8%. Jefferies' Janardan Menon, still a buyer, wrote on September 29 that he expects the cycle to peak in the fourth quarter and that valuations usually fall three to six months ahead of it.
02
AI share and China. UBS's Francois-Xavier Bouvignies (Neutral, €64) put the Q3 shortfall down to a less favourable profitability outlook from growing competition in China, and in September argued that from 2028 the market will have to digest the capacity built for AI.
03
Automotive is growing slowly. The largest segment grew 3.3% year on year, its margin fell to 18.4% from 19.8%, and weak demand for high-voltage parts for electric drivetrains offsets the software-defined-vehicle gains. Refocusing that business costs about one percentage point of Group gross margin, per the CFO.
04
Spending and debt. Capital spending (about €2.7B planned for this year) and the €2.18B Marvell automotive Ethernet purchase in fiscal 2025 left reported free cash flow at −€0.94B over the last four quarters, and net debt stood at €5.19B at June 30 — before the ~€570M ams OSRAM payment in July. If the upcycle stalls, idle-capacity costs (about €650M this year) rise rather than fall.

Notable One-Off Items

Three items distort the reported figures. First, acquisition accounting: about €400M a year of amortisation from the Cypress and Marvell automotive Ethernet purchases sits outside the Segment Result, which is why adjusted EPS (€1.47 TTM) is 60% above IFRS EPS (€0.92). Second, restructuring and the high-voltage automotive refocus: Q3 carried €203M of non-segment charges, and the refocus costs about one point of Group gross margin. Third, cash flow: the €2.18B Marvell payment in Q4 FY2025 and the ~€570M ams OSRAM payment in Q4 FY2026 sit inside reported free cash flow, so the adjusted measure the company guides on (~€1.85B for fiscal 2026) gives the cleaner view of the underlying cash generation.

Readings above 70 mean the stock has risen hard and fast relative to its own recent history ("overbought"); below 30 means it has fallen hard and fast ("oversold"). 08 Summary & Key Risks

Bull Case

Infineon is the largest supplier in the two chip markets that matter most for electrification — cars and power — and it has become a principal supplier of power delivery for AI data centers at the moment demand outruns supply. Revenue is at a record €4.17B a quarter and guided to €4.7B, the Segment Result margin is guided from 19.1% to ~23%, the backlog is about €30B, customers are prepaying to reserve capacity worth a high single-digit billion euros, and the AI revenue plan for fiscal 2027 is due a "material" upgrade on November 10. The capacity is already built: Dresden is open and Bangkok is ramping. If consensus is right about fiscal 2027 (€19.66B revenue, €2.79 adjusted EPS), the stock is on 23x earnings with margins heading back toward the 27% of the last peak — and 8 of the 10 brokers that acted since the print rate it a buy, with an average target of €86.

Bear Case

The stock doubled in three months on expectations, then fell 38% from its closing high in eight weeks before any results were published — a sign of how much of the price is sentiment about the AI cycle. Trailing IFRS earnings put it on 70x, reported free cash flow over the last four quarters was negative, and net debt was €5.19B at June 30, before the ~€570M ams OSRAM payment in July, in a year of about €2.7B capex. Q3 showed revenue beating while margins missed, and the Q4 margin guide fell short of consensus. Automotive, nearly half the business, grew 3%. Jefferies expects the chip cycle to peak in the fourth quarter, Morgan Stanley downgraded on a memory-chip peak, and UBS sees AI capacity needing digestion from 2028. If the November guide merely meets the high bar, the multiple has little support.

Top 3 Risks

01
A cycle peak priced as a new plateau. Consensus has fiscal-2027 revenue up 20% and adjusted EPS up 58% (€1.77 → €2.79). Three brokers — Jefferies, Morgan Stanley and UBS — have each written about a peak or digestion phase within the next two years. Jefferies notes that valuations typically fall three to six months before the peak.
02
Margin delivery. Q3's Segment Result missed consensus by 1.5% despite a revenue beat; Green Industrial Power's margin fell to 9.8% on what management calls temporary effects. The Q4 guide of ~23% requires a four-point step-up in one quarter, helped by price increases in AI-related products and partly offset by rising input costs, per management.
03
China and the electric-car slowdown. Weak demand for high-voltage drivetrain parts is costing Automotive margin, and UBS links the profitability outlook to rising competition in China — while management sees supply constraints in China's automotive-grade chip market as an opportunity. Which of those dominates shows up in Automotive's growth rate.
Industry Commentary
AI data centers are rewiring power delivery
STRUCTURAL TAILWIND

Ever-growing processing capability needs energy-efficient power delivery, and every conversion step between the grid and the chip wastes energy. That pushes operators toward wide-bandgap materials — silicon carbide for the high-voltage grid-to-rack stages, gallium nitride for dense intermediate stages — and toward suppliers that can deliver the whole chain at volume. Infineon cites a Gartner report on AI data-center power semiconductors that called it "the company to beat". The result shows in PSS: revenue +34% year on year and a 24.9% margin. Management's next leg is agentic AI, whose inference workloads raise CPU power demand, an area where it claims leadership; the size of that opportunity will be part of the November AI revenue update.

Where in the chip cycle are we?
KEY DEBATE

Management's reading on August 5 was an upcycle still broadening: inventories largely run down and lead times rising — a typical early-upcycle pattern, in the CEO's description — while cancellation rates, escalation calls and customers' willingness to pay surcharges for faster delivery point to underlying demand strength. The sell side is split on timing rather than direction. Jefferies (September 29) expects most European chipmakers to meet expectations but guidance to rise little, with a cycle peak in the fourth quarter; Morgan Stanley (September 8) sees memory chips nearing their peak; Berenberg (September 8) argues the semiconductor investment cycle should run beyond 2028, pointing to sustained high spending at TSMC. Infineon's fiscal year ends in September, so on November 10 it will be among the first chipmakers to give a 2027 outlook.

Cars: more chips per vehicle, fewer vehicles
MIXED

Global light-vehicle output is forecast at about 91 million units for 2026, roughly 2% lower than 2025, so automotive chipmakers grow only through content. Software-defined vehicles — central computers, zone controllers and Ethernet in place of dozens of separate boxes — are the content driver, and Infineon reports design wins in that architecture with a global carmaker's software unit and in China. Electric drivetrains, once the main growth engine for power chips, are the drag: Infineon is refocusing its high-voltage business. Bernstein (September 7) sees an automotive recovery starting across the chip sector, with better pricing an underrated lever; UBS came away from a meeting with the head of Infineon's automotive division more optimistic about 2027.
