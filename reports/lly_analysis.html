01 Company Overview

Eli Lilly is a 150-year-old Indianapolis pharmaceutical company that has become the dominant force in the fastest-growing drug market in history: incretin therapies (GLP-1 class drugs) for diabetes and obesity. Full-year 2025 revenue reached $65.2B, up 45% YoY, driven by tirzepatide — sold as Mounjaro ($23.0B, +99%) for type 2 diabetes and Zepbound ($13.5B, +175%) for obesity — and management raised 2026 guidance to $85–87B after a Q2 2026 beat ($23.0B, +48% YoY). At roughly $1.10 trillion in market cap, Lilly is the most valuable healthcare company ever, worth more than the next several large-cap pharma peers combined (Johnson & Johnson, the #2 pharma, sits near $626B).

03 Competitive Moat & Vulnerabilities
Sources of Moat
INCRETIN DUOPOLY BEST-IN-CLASS EFFICACY $50B+ MANUFACTURING SCALE PIPELINE DEPTH (RETATRUTIDE) PATENT RUNWAY TO ~2036 LILLYDIRECT CHANNEL ORAL SMALL-MOLECULE COST EDGE

Lilly's moat is a rare pharma triple-lock: molecule superiority, manufacturing capacity, and distribution. Tirzepatide beat semaglutide head-to-head (SURMOUNT-5: ~20% vs ~14% weight loss), and that efficacy gap has translated into share — Lilly's incretin franchise generated ~$14.9B in Q2 2026 versus roughly $8.3B for Novo Nordisk's entire GLP-1 portfolio, roughly 64% of duopoly revenue, and tirzepatide now leads US prescriptions. Behind it sits a capital moat competitors cannot quickly replicate: more than $50B committed to US manufacturing since 2020 (four new sites announced in Feb 2025 alone), which ended the shortage era and lets Lilly flood channels — including its direct-to-consumer LillyDirect pharmacy — while rivals ration. Foundayo (orforglipron) adds a chemistry moat: as a synthetic small molecule (not a peptide), it is dramatically cheaper to mass-produce and needs no cold chain, positioning Lilly for price-elastic and international markets. Compound-exclusivity on tirzepatide runs to roughly 2036, and the pipeline (retatrutide's 22.6% Phase 3 weight loss, bimagrumab for muscle preservation) is designed to leapfrog its own franchise before anyone else does.

Key Vulnerabilities
▸Single-franchise concentration. Incretins are ~74% of revenue and effectively all of the growth. Any tirzepatide safety signal, a superior competitor molecule, or faster-than-modeled patient discontinuation (real-world dropout within 12 months is high across the class) hits the entire equity story at a ~41x trailing multiple.
▸Pricing and policy compression. Net realized price fell ~7–10% in early 2026. The TrumpRx/most-favored-nation deals (Nov 2025) cap direct-to-consumer prices, Novo cuts wholesale prices to a flat $675 in Jan 2027, and tirzepatide is in the queue for Medicare price negotiation. Volume must keep outrunning price — HSBC's March 2026 downgrade argued it won't.
▸Competitive wave 2027–2029. Novo's CagriSema (~22.7% weight loss, FDA filing late 2026), Amgen's monthly MariTide (Phase 3), Viking's VK2735, and Pfizer's Metsera-acquired amylin/GLP-1 assets all target Lilly's share and pricing umbrella. Oral semaglutide already launched ahead of Foundayo.
▸Execution/capex risk. A $50B+ manufacturing buildout, ~$12B annual capex and serial IPR&D charges ($2.8B in Q2'26 alone) assume the demand curve holds; tariff policy and API supply chains add further operational tail risk.

Notable One-Off Items

Q2 2026 GAAP and non-GAAP EPS both absorbed $3.03/share of acquired IPR&D charges ($2.8B), mainly from the Orna Therapeutics and Ajax deals — Lilly expenses deal-related R&D immediately, so trailing P/E overstates the "clean" multiple. Q2 also included $703M of restructuring/integration costs and ~$445M of net investment gains. FY2025 carried $2.9B of IPR&D charges. Separately, ~$12B/yr of manufacturing capex (part of a $50B+ US buildout) suppresses free cash flow relative to net income. None of these change the underlying trajectory, but all of them distort single-period comparisons.

08 Summary & Key Risks

▲ Bull Case

Lilly owns ~64% of a duopoly attacking a market consensus still sizes at $150B+, growing revenue ~48% with 85%+ gross margins, and its own pipeline (Foundayo scaling, retatrutide at 22.6% weight loss filing Q1 2027) is the most likely thing to obsolete tirzepatide. At a 1.44 PEG — cheaper than the S&P 500 growth-adjusted — two more beat-and-raise quarters and a clean Foundayo ramp push the stock through the $1,600 street-high target.

▼ Bear Case

74% of revenue rides one mechanism whose net price is falling 7–10%/yr into a 2027 pricing reset (Novo's $675 flat price, MFN caps, Medicare negotiation) just as CagriSema, MariTide and VK2735 arrive. If HSBC's $80–120B TAM is right, a 41x trailing / ~30x forward multiple on decelerating volume growth compresses toward pharma norms — and the Aug 2025 episode showed this stock can lose 14% in a day on a single trial readout.

Top 3 Risks
01Incretin concentration + pricing compression. One franchise is ~74% of revenue while net price declines accelerate into the Jan 2027 reset; if volume growth slows before oncology/immunology/neuro can matter, the premium multiple unwinds quickly.
02Competitive leapfrog, 2027–2029. CagriSema, MariTide (monthly dosing), VK2735, oral semaglutide and Pfizer/Metsera assets all target the same patients; any rival showing tirzepatide-class efficacy with better tolerability or dosing resets share and price assumptions.
03Binary pipeline/regulatory events. The stock trades on trial readouts and policy headlines — a retatrutide safety surprise, a Foundayo script stall, or an adverse Medicare negotiation outcome are each single-event, double-digit-percent risks (in both directions).
  Industry Commentary: The GLP-1 Macro Picture
The Great GLP-1 Pricing Reset
ONGOING HEADWIND

The shortage-era pricing umbrella is folding. Lilly's net realized price fell ~7% in Q1 2026 (~10% underlying); Wegovy's US sales dropped 22% YoY in Q2 2026 on price despite higher volumes; and from Jan 1, 2027 Novo moves to a flat $675 wholesale price (−50% on Wegovy, −35% on Ozempic). Layer on the Nov 2025 TrumpRx/most-favored-nation framework — cash-pay tiers starting around $245–499/month and expanded Medicare/Medicaid obesity coverage in exchange for price concessions — plus tirzepatide's spot in the Medicare negotiation queue, and the class is converting from luxury-priced scarcity to mass-market volume. Lilly's Q2'26 numbers show volume winning so far (incretin revenue +75% YoY against falling prices), but every future model now hinges on that ratio holding.

How Big Is Obesity, Really? The TAM Debate
CRITICAL CONSTRAINT

Roughly a billion people worldwide live with obesity, but bulls and bears disagree by nearly 2x on what that monetizes to: consensus models $150B+ in annual incretin revenue by the early 2030s, while HSBC's March 2026 downgrade argued $80–120B through 2032, citing price wars and real-world discontinuation (a large share of patients stop within a year). The swing factors: oral pills (Foundayo, Wegovy pill — already >265K US weekly scripts) opening price-sensitive and international markets; label expansion into sleep apnea, heart failure, kidney disease, MASH and Alzheimer's prevention; and insurance coverage, where the mid-2026 Medicare/Medicaid obesity expansion is the largest single access unlock to date. Where reality lands in that $80–150B range is, mechanically, the LLY share price.

Manufacturing as Strategy — and the 2027 Competitive Wave
EMERGING TAILWIND

The defining industry lesson of 2023–2025 was that in incretins, capacity is market share — and Lilly out-built everyone, committing $50B+ to US manufacturing while tariff policy (Section 232) makes domestic capacity a regulatory asset too. For bulls: that installed base plus a small-molecule pill and a superior late-stage pipeline (retatrutide, bimagrumab) means Lilly converts the price reset into a volume land-grab, compounding EPS 30%+ while the 2027 entrants fight for second place. For bears: $12B/yr of capex, a leveraged balance sheet (D/E 1.62) and a 41x multiple assume the demand curve and Lilly's ~64% share both survive CagriSema, MariTide, VK2735 and a flood of cheaper orals — a bet with very little room for a single bad readout. The duopoly is safe through ~2027; after that, the moat gets stress-tested annually.
