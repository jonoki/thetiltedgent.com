01 Company Overview

Kweichow Moutai makes baijiu, the sorghum spirit that is China's banquet and gift drink, in the town of Maotai in Guizhou province. Its flagship liquor, Kweichow Moutai — best known as the 53% Feitian 500ml bottle — is in the company's own words "the originator and archetype of Moutai-flavor Baijiu" (2025 annual report). It is one of the most profitable consumer businesses anywhere: in FY2025 the flagship Moutai liquor line booked CNY 146.50B of revenue at a 93.53% gross margin, and the group earned CNY 82.32B of net profit on CNY 168.84B of revenue — about 49 yuan of profit for every 100 yuan of sales. At the September 30, 2026 close it was worth CNY 1,573.38B (about $234.73B), and the Guizhou provincial government controls it: the actual controller is the Guizhou SASAC, through China Kweichow Moutai Distillery (Group), which held 54.50% at June 30, 2026.

The story now is a company rebuilding how it sells its own product. FY2025 was a down year — revenue −1.21%, net profit −4.53%, EPS CNY 65.66 against 68.64 — and in January 2026 the board adopted a market-oriented operating plan: a pyramid of products, a dynamic retail price that moves with the market, and a shift from selling mainly through distributors to selling direct through the company's i Moutai app and its own stores. Twice in 2026 Moutai raised what distributors pay for a bottle of Feitian: from CNY 1,169 to 1,269 on March 31, then to 1,369 on July 18. The first half shows both sides of the trade. Revenue rose 1.47% to CNY 90.70B, direct sales jumped 29.9% to 57% of liquor revenue and i Moutai alone brought in CNY 40.26B, up 274% — while wholesale fell 21.6%, the second quarter on its own shrank (revenue −5.1%, net profit −6.9%) and H1 net profit slipped 1.95% to CNY 44.52B. The shares closed at CNY 1,258.62, down 8.6% in 2026 and 38.6% below the December 2021 month-end high of CNY 2,050.00, while the market waits to see whether the new price system holds when demand is weak. Management's answer at its August 21 results briefing, by the company's own account, was that sales and the core price system are stable, that both Moutai and series liquor sold more by volume, and that it is drafting a market-value management plan for 2027–2029.

03 Competitive Moat & Vulnerabilities

Sources of moat:

Geographical-indication terroir 340,000 t ageing in the cellar 93.5% gross margin on the flagship Pricing power over the trade Direct channel (i Moutai) No borrowings State-backed ownership

Moutai's moat is that nobody else can make Moutai. The liquor is a national geographical-indication product tied to one valley of the Chishui River, made through a year-long cycle of fermentation and distillation and then aged for years; the cellar held 339,978 tonnes at the end of 2025, more than three years of 2025's sales volume. That scarcity is why the brand earns a 93.53% gross margin on the flagship and why, even in a weak market, it could raise the price distributors pay twice in four months. The vulnerability is that much of the demand is gifting and business entertaining, so it moves with official austerity, the property cycle and business confidence — and that the end price was historically set by a resale market the company did not control. The 2026 reform is an attempt to take that price back.

Key Vulnerabilities

01
An industry in a downturn. The H1 2026 report describes baijiu as going through a deep cyclical and structural adjustment, with competition for a market that is no longer growing and the strongest brands taking share. The CSI Liquor Index fell 36.2% in the twelve months to September 30, 2026 and is down 63.1% from its September 2021 level. Moutai has held up far better than the sector, but its own revenue has now fallen on a trailing-twelve-month basis (−4.6%).
02
The reform costs money before it pays. Moving bottles from distributors to the app shrank wholesale revenue 21.6% in H1 2026, cut distributor prepayments by 60%, and coincided with a weaker second quarter. If retail demand at the new prices proves thinner than the distributor system's orders were, the company will have traded volume for control.
03
Governance runs through the provincial government. The chairman was recommended by the Guizhou government and arrived from the provincial energy administration; the finance chief was detained by an anti-graft commission in March 2026; the general manager has been "acting" since 2023. The company says operations are normal and the detention will not have a material impact, but minority holders own a business whose leadership is set by the state.
04
The group bank inside the accounts. A consolidated subsidiary, Kweichow Moutai Group Finance Co., takes deposits from other parent-group companies (CNY 25.43B at June 30, 2026, +41% in the half). Its flows swing reported operating cash flow — +438.8% in H1 2026, −33.5% in FY2025 — so the cash-flow line says little about the liquor business from one period to the next.

Notable One-Off Items

Three things distort the reported figures. First, the finance subsidiary: H1 2026 operating cash flow of CNY 70.69B (+438.8%) came mainly from more deposits from parent-group companies and fewer locked interbank deposits, not from selling more liquor. Second, share cancellations: Moutai finished its second buyback on May 27, 2026 — 2,188,614 shares for CNY 3.00B at an average CNY 1,370.70 — and cancelled them, cutting the share count 0.17% to 1,250,081,601; the first, CNY 6.0B round was completed in 2025. Third, accounting: an August 2026 change to adopt two new Ministry of Finance interpretations, which the company says has no material effect. Non-recurring items are small: H1 2026 EPS excluding them was CNY 35.53 against 35.57 reported.

⚠ STATIC DATA AS OF SEP 30, 2026
RSI measures how one-sided recent price moves have been on a 0–100 scale. Readings above 70 mean the stock has risen hard and fast relative to its own recent history ("overbought"); below 30 means it has fallen hard and fast ("oversold"). It is a measure of momentum, not of value — a cheap stock can stay oversold for a long time.

From the September 2021 month-end close of CNY 1,830.00 to the September 30, 2026 close of CNY 1,258.62, Moutai lost 31.2% of its price. Adding back the CNY 223.01 a share of dividends paid over the window (Yahoo dividend history; not reinvested) narrows the loss to about 19%. The shape is a long, uneven slide rather than a crash: a high of CNY 2,050.00 at the end of December 2021, a sharp break in October 2022 (−27.9% in the month, the worst of the series) that was mostly recovered within three months, three years of drift between roughly CNY 1,400 and 1,900, and then a 2026 low of CNY 1,185.49 in June — the lowest month-end in the five years — before the July price rise lifted it.

The 6-period monthly RSI printed 18.4 in June 2026, the most oversold reading in the series and lower than the October 2022 trough (23.6) and the mid-2024 lows (23.9 in July 2024). It recovered to 46.7 in July and has drifted back to 37.3, on the weak side of neutral. The 10-month moving average, about CNY 1,348.82, sits above the price: the trend is still down, and the July rally faded through August and September.

08 Summary & Key Risks

Bull Case

You pay 19.35x trailing earnings for a business with a 93.5% gross margin on its flagship, no borrowings, a 4.13% dividend yield, a written promise to pay out at least 75% of 2026 profit, and 340,000 tonnes of irreplaceable liquor ageing in the cellar. The 2026 reform moves the margin that distributors used to earn — the gap between a CNY 1,369 contract price and a CNY 1,639 retail price — into Moutai's own app, and i Moutai already sells 44% of the liquor. The two price rises show the brand can still set its price in a downturn. Brokers expect earnings to grow again in 2026 (average EPS CNY 67.55, +2.9%) and 2027 (+6.3%). If baijiu demand stabilises, a company that held its profit nearly flat through the industry's worst stretch should not trade on a P/E in line with an index that has fallen 63% in five years.

Bear Case

Profit has now fallen for a year and a half: FY2025 net profit −4.53%, H1 2026 −1.95%, and the second quarter −6.9% even after a price rise. Trailing revenue is down 4.6%, gross margin fell 1.7 points in the half, and the distributor channel shrank 21.6%. The price increases may be reaching for revenue that demand will not supply, and the industry the company describes as in deep adjustment is still falling — the CSI Liquor Index is down 36% in a year. A 47% P/E premium to the CSI 300 is not cheap for a business with no growth. Leadership is appointed by the provincial government, the finance chief was detained in March, and the general manager has been "acting" for three years. A bear also reads the generous dividend as serving a state owner that wants cash.

Top 3 Risks

01
Demand for gifting and banquet liquor stays weak. Revenue has fallen on a trailing basis and the second quarter shrank. If the Q3 report shows the July price rise cost volume, the reform will look like price-taking in a falling market rather than price-setting.
02
The channel shift goes wrong. Distributors carried inventory and prepaid for it; the app does not. With wholesale revenue down 21.6% and prepayments down 60%, Moutai now absorbs swings in demand that the trade used to buffer, and a fall in resale prices would hit the company's own retail price directly.
03
State control and governance events. A chairman change in late 2025, a detained CFO in 2026 and a long-acting general manager all came through the provincial government. Its priorities — dividends, local employment, industrial policy — need not match a minority shareholder's.
Industry Commentary
Baijiu in a down cycle
CRITICAL CONSTRAINT

The H1 2026 report opens its industry discussion by saying baijiu is undergoing a deep cyclical and structural adjustment, in which competition for a fixed pool of demand and the pull of the strongest brands will become more pronounced. The market agrees: the CSI Liquor Index stood at 6,228.90 on September 30, 2026, against 9,768.08 a year earlier and 16,869.66 five years earlier, and Wuliangye, the number two, closed at CNY 70.06 for a market value of CNY 271.95B. Moutai's revenue held near CNY 170B through this while peers' valuations collapsed, which fits what the 2025 annual report calls the industry's "Matthew Effect": as baijiu adjusts, resources flow toward the stronger companies and leading brands. The CSI Liquor Index now trades on 19.15x earnings, almost exactly Moutai's 19.35x.

Who sets the price of a bottle
ONGOING SHIFT

For years Moutai sold Feitian to distributors at a fixed factory price far below what bottles fetched on the street, so the scarcity premium went to the trade. The 2026 plan reverses that: the company sets a self-operated retail price that, in its 2025 annual report's words, comes from "a market-oriented dynamic pricing adjustment mechanism" meant "to maintain relative stability", sells more through i Moutai, and raises the contract price when the market allows. On July 18 the contract price was CNY 1,369 and the app price CNY 1,639. The test is whether the company can keep a price in a falling market that distributors could not; the second quarter (revenue −5.1%) was the first hard reading, and the third will be the first under the July price.

A state-owned cash machine
SHAREHOLDER RETURNS

Moutai paid CNY 65.03B of cash dividends for FY2025 — 79.0% of net profit — and spent CNY 6.0B on its first buyback and CNY 3.0B on its second, cancelling the shares. Its controlling shareholder bought CNY 3.0B of stock between September 1 and December 26, 2025. Cash dividends for FY2023–2025 totalled CNY 192.49B, 237% of the average annual net profit of those three years (the company's April 17, 2026 dividend notice; the ratio is our calculation). With no borrowings and CNY 53.52B of cash and CNY 141.08B lent to other banks at June 30, 2026, the payout is not under strain. The bull reading is that a 4.13% yield is paid while the reform plays out; the bear reading is that the state owner will keep asking for it.
