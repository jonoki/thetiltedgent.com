01 Company Overview

Sea Limited is three businesses under one founder: Shopee, a mobile-first e-commerce marketplace in Southeast Asia that also operates in Brazil; Monee, a consumer and small-business lender and payments arm; and Garena, a mobile-games publisher whose franchise is Free Fire. FY2025 revenue was $22.94B, up 36.4%, with net income of $1.58B against $447.8M in FY2024 and adjusted EBITDA of $3.44B, up 75.2%. In Q3 2026 (quarter ended September 30) revenue rose 43.4% to $8.58B, net income 34.0% to $502.5M and adjusted EBITDA 28.9% to $931.3M. The market value is $46.16B (612.48M shares × the $75.36 close). Sea has no Singapore listing and is not a constituent of the Straits Times Index.

The premise usually attached to this stock is that Garena's cash funds Shopee. That was the architecture of 2019–2022, when Shopee burned money and the games business paid for it. It is no longer an accurate description. Shopee has been EBITDA-positive for two years — $880.6M of adjusted EBITDA in FY2025 and $269.2M in Q3 2026 alone, which the CEO called a new record. What is still true is that Garena remains the largest single profit pool: $1.66B of FY2025 adjusted EBITDA, 48% of the group total, on only 10.5% of revenue, and $458.2M of Q3 2026's $931.3M. The capital question has moved. The business that now consumes balance sheet is Monee, whose loans outstanding grew 60.0% year over year to $12.6B in Q3 2026. That — the profitability of each segment and the quality of a fast-growing loan book — is what this report leads with, plus a new item: Sea Cloud, an AI cloud business the founder announced on October 20, 2026.

The stock is a study in round trips: $343.57 at the October 2021 month-end, $36.22 at the November 2023 month-end (−89.5%), $186.54 in August 2025, and $75.36 now, 59.6% below that high. The legs down came on March 3, 2026, when the FY2025 results and a guide for Shopee EBITDA merely "no lower than" 2025 took the stock down 16.4% on the day, and October 20, 2026, when it fell 9.09% ($88.47 → $80.43) after the Sea Cloud announcement.

03 Competitive Moat & Vulnerabilities

Sources of moat:

Marketplace scale ($127.4B GMV) Own logistics network Checkout-embedded credit data Free Fire user base Net cash $4.66B Founder control, long horizon

The moat comes from the three businesses reinforcing each other. Shopee gives Monee what most consumer lenders in the region lack: years of purchase, payment and delivery data on each borrower, captured at checkout, where the credit product is offered at the moment of need. Monee's credit in turn raises Shopee's conversion and basket size. Management's claim that "each improvement helps us serve more users … and reach further beyond Shopee" is the thesis. Profitability gives scale a hard edge: with Shopee, Monee and Garena all EBITDA-positive and $4.66B of net cash, Sea can outspend rivals on subsidies and still report a profit. The weaknesses: a marketplace's advantage is only as durable as its sellers' loyalty, and the lending edge has only been tested through a benign credit cycle.

Key Vulnerabilities

01
Credit quality has not been tested by a downturn. The on-book loan book ($11.2B) is now larger than cash plus short-term investments ($8.02B, September 30, 2026). A 1.3% NPL ratio on a book growing 60% a year is partly an artefact of how new the loans are. Provisions growing faster than the book (82.3% vs 60.0%) and the NPL ratio rising from 1.0% are the early signals, and both moved the wrong way in Q3.
02
Shopee's margin is a policy choice, and competitors get a vote. The 2026 guide traded EBITDA growth for GMV growth; commentary around the March sell-off cited competition from TikTok Shop and incremental ecosystem investment, and DealStreetAsia reported that TikTok Shop launched a standalone shopping app in November in Singapore, Malaysia, Thailand and the Philippines. If rivals force more subsidy spending, the $1B EBITDA target slips back toward the $880.6M floor. Asked when peak spending ends, Chris Feng said "It's a bit hard to argue when is the peak investment as we roll out this program in different countries" (call transcript, Singju Post).
03
Garena is one hit deep. Almost half of group adjusted EBITDA depends on a single mobile title, Free Fire, whose active users were flat year over year in Q3 (671.7M vs 670.8M) while bookings grew only 5.9%. The two new licensed-IP games are unproven.
04
Outside shareholders cannot outvote the founder. At 15 votes per Class B share, Forrest Li holds 57.6% of votes on 16.0% of the equity, and the board votes Tencent's 8.3% by proxy. Outside shareholders cannot change strategy or management; the investment is a bet on one person's capital allocation. The Sea Cloud push below is such a decision.
05
Sea Cloud is a new, unproven spending line. Founder Forrest Li's October 20, 2026 email to employees announced an AI cloud business, and the stock fell 9.09% that day. On the call he said "this is at a very, very early stage," "we haven't secured any enterprise contracts" and "we do not expect Sea Cloud to have a significant impact on our financials for now," and that Sea will "only pursue opportunities with clear customer commitments and attractive returns," mainly funded by "partners and customer prepayments" (call transcript, Singju Post). No capital-spending figure has been given. Investors' worry is the cost of a business with a different profile from the three Sea already runs.

Notable One-Off Items

Two items distort naive comparisons. First, the adjusted EBITDA definition changed: starting with the Q3 2026 release, Sea no longer includes the net change in Garena's deferred revenue in adjusted EBITDA (it added $454.7M in FY2025) and discloses it separately instead ($52.0M in Q3 2026, $151.9M a year earlier). Garena's and the group's EBITDA are not comparable with the FY2025 figures above. Second, adjusted EBITDA excludes share-based compensation ($625.0M in FY2025), which is a real cost to shareholders.

It is a measure of momentum, not of value — a cheap stock can stay oversold for a long time.

From the September 2021 month-end close of $318.73 to the November 13, 2026 settled close of $75.36, SE lost 76.4% of its price; it pays no dividend, so that is also the total return. The ADSs have traded on the NYSE since October 20, 2017, so the full five-year window is charted. 08 Summary & Key Risks

Bull Case

All three segments are EBITDA-positive, and the group grew revenue 43% in Q3 with $4.66B of net cash. Shopee posted a record $269.2M of adjusted EBITDA (+44.7%) and is raising its take rate (core marketplace revenue +50.6% on GMV +23.3%); management reaffirmed GMV growth of around 25% and $1 billion of adjusted EBITDA for 2026. Monee lends on proprietary checkout data and still grows its book 60.0%. Garena's adjusted EBITDA rose 45.9%. At 20.6x FY2026 consensus earnings, with 26 of 28 analysts at Buy, the shares are 59.6% below their August 2025 high while profit kept rising.

Bear Case

Quarterly revenue grew 43.4% but adjusted EBITDA only 28.9%: growth is being bought with marketing and credit costs. Monee's provisions rose 82.3% against a loan book up 60.0%, the 90-day NPL ratio rose from 1.0% to 1.3%, and on-book loans ($11.2B) exceed cash and short-term investments ($8.02B). Garena's bookings grew only 5.9% with flat active users, yet it earns nearly half the group's adjusted EBITDA. The founder, who holds 57.6% of the votes on 16.0% of the equity, has now announced Sea Cloud, a new AI cloud business with no customer contracts and no stated spending, and minority holders cannot block it.

Monee credit losses. A $12.6B book growing 60% a year across emerging-market consumers has not been through a recession. Industry Commentary
Premise check: "Garena's cash funds Shopee"
OUTDATED — PARTLY TRUE

The premise was accurate in 2019–2022 and is not now. What is still true: Garena is the largest single profit contributor — $1,656.1M of FY2025 adjusted EBITDA (48.2%) and $458.2M in Q3 2026 (49.2%). What is no longer true: Shopee does not need funding. It generated $880.6M of adjusted EBITDA in FY2025 (from $155.8M in FY2024) and $269.2M in Q3 2026, and the group produced $3.99B of trailing free cash flow (StockAnalysis, TTM). Where the capital actually goes now: Monee. Its loans outstanding rose from $9.2B at December 31, 2025 to $12.6B at September 30, 2026. The better one-line description of Sea in 2026: Garena and Shopee generate the profits; Monee uses the balance sheet.

Embedded lending in emerging-market e-commerce
CYCLE RISK

The Shopee–Monee model — credit offered at checkout, underwritten on the platform's own transaction data — is also the model of MercadoLibre's Mercado Pago in Latin America, and Shopee now competes with MercadoLibre in Brazil. It produces fast growth and good early credit metrics because the lender can see the borrower's behaviour and offers the loan at the point of sale. Its untested property is correlation: the same consumer weakness that cuts GMV also raises defaults, so marketplace revenue and credit losses deteriorate together. Sea's NPL ratio of 1.0% at June 30 and management's claim in the Q2 release that its "advances in … risk capabilities are compounding" are the bull evidence; provisions outgrowing the book are the bear evidence. The Q3 print (Nov 10) showed NPLs at 1.3% and provisions up 82.3% against loans up 60.0%; Q4 is the next data point.

From growth-at-all-costs to disciplined reinvestment
EMERGING TAILWIND

Sea spent 2022–23 proving it could turn a profit and 2024–25 proving the profits could scale (group adjusted EBITDA $1.96B → $3.44B). 2026 is the first year it has chosen to reinvest from a position of profitability rather than from Garena's cash, with a management-set floor on Shopee EBITDA. The market punished that choice in March. The bull framing: a founder-controlled company with net cash, reinvesting at 25%+ GMV growth into a rising take rate, deserves a growth multiple. The bear framing: founder control is exactly why the 2021 overexpansion happened, and "reinvestment" is the same word used then. How to decide: segment EBITDA growth converging back toward revenue growth over the next four quarters. It was 28.9% vs 43.4% in Q3.
