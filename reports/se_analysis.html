01 Company Overview

Sea Limited is three businesses under one founder: Shopee, a mobile-first e-commerce marketplace in Southeast Asia that also operates in Brazil; Monee, a consumer and small-business lender and payments arm; and Garena, a mobile-games publisher whose franchise is Free Fire. FY2025 revenue was $22.94B, up 36.4%, with net income of $1.58B against $447.8M in FY2024 and adjusted EBITDA of $3.44B, up 75.2%. At a $62.87B market value it ranks #398 in the world and #3 among Singapore-headquartered companies by companiesmarketcap's country ranking — behind DBS and OCBC, ahead of Singtel — although it has no Singapore listing and is not a constituent of the Straits Times Index.

The premise usually attached to this stock is that Garena's cash funds Shopee. That was the architecture of 2019–2022, when Shopee burned money and the games business paid for it. It is no longer an accurate description. Shopee has been EBITDA-positive for two years — $880.6M of adjusted EBITDA in FY2025 and $255.4M in Q2 2026 alone. What is still true is that Garena remains the largest single profit pool: $1.66B of FY2025 adjusted EBITDA, 48% of the group total, on only 10.5% of revenue. The capital question has moved. The business that now consumes balance sheet is Monee, whose loan book grew 62.5% year over year to $11.1B in Q2 2026. That — the profitability pivot by segment and the quality of a fast-growing loan book — is what this report leads with.

The stock is a study in round trips: $343.57 at the October 2021 month-end, $36.22 at the November 2023 month-end (−89.5%), $186.54 in August 2025, and $102.65 now, 45% below that high. The most recent leg down came on March 3, 2026, when the FY2025 results and a guide for Shopee EBITDA merely "no lower than" 2025 took the stock down 16.4% on the day.

03 Competitive Moat & Vulnerabilities

Sources of moat:

Marketplace scale ($127.4B GMV) Own logistics network Checkout-embedded credit data Free Fire user base Net cash $4.97B Founder control, long horizon

The moat comes from the three businesses reinforcing each other. Shopee gives Monee what most consumer lenders in the region lack: years of purchase, payment and delivery data on each borrower, captured at checkout, where the credit product is offered at the moment of need. Monee's credit in turn raises Shopee's conversion and basket size. Management's claim that "each improvement helps us serve more users … and reach further beyond Shopee" is the thesis. Profitability gives scale a hard edge: with Shopee, Monee and Garena all EBITDA-positive and $4.97B of net cash, Sea can outspend rivals on subsidies and still report a profit. The weaknesses: a marketplace's advantage is only as durable as its sellers' loyalty, and the lending edge has only been tested through a benign credit cycle.

Key Vulnerabilities

01
Credit quality has not been tested by a downturn. The on-book loan book ($10.0B) is now larger than cash plus short-term investments ($9.10B). A 1.0% NPL ratio on a book growing 60% a year is partly an artefact of how new the loans are. Provisions growing faster than the book (71.5% vs 62.5%) is the early signal to watch.
02
Shopee's margin is a policy choice, and competitors get a vote. The 2026 guide traded EBITDA growth for GMV growth; commentary around the March sell-off cited competition from TikTok Shop and incremental ecosystem investment. If rivals force more subsidy spending, the $1B EBITDA ambition slips back toward the $880.6M floor.
03
Garena is one hit deep. Almost half of group adjusted EBITDA depends on a single mobile title, Free Fire, whose active users were flat year over year in Q2 (666.3M vs 664.8M). The two new licensed-IP games are unproven.
04
Outside shareholders cannot outvote the founder. At 15 votes per Class B share, Forrest Li holds 57.6% of votes on 16.0% of the equity, and the board votes Tencent's 8.3% by proxy. Outside shareholders cannot change strategy or management; the investment is a bet on one person's capital allocation. Meanwhile the share count rose 2.32% year over year despite buybacks.

Notable One-Off Items

Three items distort naive comparisons. First, the adjusted EBITDA definition is changing: starting with the Q3 2026 release, Sea will stop including the net change in Garena's deferred revenue in adjusted EBITDA (it added $454.7M in FY2025) and will disclose it separately instead. Garena and group EBITDA will step down with no change in the business. Second, adjusted EBITDA excludes share-based compensation ($625.0M in FY2025), which is a real cost to shareholders, and the share count grew 2.32% year over year despite buybacks. Third, FX translation: accumulated other comprehensive loss widened from $4.8M at December 31, 2025 to $198.7M at June 30, 2026 — local-currency weakness against the dollar hitting the translated balance sheet. No large write-down or litigation charge appeared in the sources fetched.

RSI measures how one-sided recent price moves have been on a 0–100 scale. Readings above 70 mean the stock has risen hard and fast relative to its own recent history ("overbought"); below 30 means it has fallen hard and fast ("oversold"). 08 Summary & Key Risks

Bull Case

All three segments are now EBITDA-positive, and the group grows revenue 48% with $4.97B of net cash. Shopee is raising its take rate (core marketplace revenue +65.6% on GMV +28.4%) and choosing to reinvest rather than harvest; the CEO is already signalling $1B of EBITDA against an $880.6M floor. Monee lends on proprietary checkout data at a 1.0% NPL ratio and still grows its book 62.5%. Garena turns 56% of bookings into EBITDA. At 23.85x forward earnings, with 28 of 29 analysts at Buy and every published target above the price, the market is pricing the 2026 reinvestment year as if it were permanent.

Bear Case

Quarterly revenue +48% but adjusted EBITDA and net income both only +10.6% is not operating leverage — it is growth being bought. The fastest-growing line is a consumer lender whose provisions are outrunning its loan book, whose NPL ratio is flattered by how new the loans are, and whose on-book loans now exceed the group's cash. Nearly half the profit rests on one mobile game with flat users, and the EBITDA definition that flatters it is being tightened next quarter. The founder holds 57.6% of the votes on 16.0% of the equity, so minority holders cannot block the next spending cycle.

Top 3 Risks

01
Monee credit losses. An $11.1B book growing 60%+ a year across emerging-market consumers has not been through a recession. Provisions already grew faster than loans in Q2 2026 (71.5% vs 62.5%); a rise in the 90-day NPL ratio from 1.0% would hit both Monee's EBITDA and the group's capital position.
02
E-commerce competition forcing a longer reinvestment cycle. If rivals (coverage cites TikTok Shop; MercadoLibre in Brazil) force Shopee to keep subsidising, 2026's EBITDA-for-GMV trade becomes 2027's too, and the multiple compresses further.
03
Garena single-title dependence. About 47–48% of group adjusted EBITDA depends on Free Fire; any decline in its engagement goes straight to the group's largest profit pool, and the new titles are unproven.

Premise check: "Garena's cash funds Shopee"
OUTDATED — PARTLY TRUE

The premise was accurate in 2019–2022 and is not now. What is still true: Garena is the largest single profit contributor — $1,656.1M of FY2025 adjusted EBITDA (48.2%) and $429.8M in Q2 2026 (46.9%). What is no longer true: Shopee does not need funding. It generated $880.6M of adjusted EBITDA in FY2025 (from $155.8M in FY2024) and $255.4M in Q2 2026, and the group produced $4.21B of trailing free cash flow. Where the capital actually goes now: Monee. Its on-book loans rose from $8.2B to $10.0B in the first half of 2026 alone. The better one-line description of Sea in 2026: Garena and Shopee generate the profits; Monee uses the balance sheet.

Embedded lending in emerging-market e-commerce
CYCLE RISK

The Shopee–Monee model — credit offered at checkout, underwritten on the platform's own transaction data — is also the model of MercadoLibre's Mercado Pago in Latin America, and Shopee now competes with MercadoLibre in Brazil. It produces fast growth and good early credit metrics because the lender can see the borrower's behaviour and offers the loan at the point of sale. Its untested property is correlation: the same consumer weakness that cuts GMV also raises defaults, so marketplace revenue and credit losses deteriorate together. Sea's 1.0% NPL ratio and management's claim that its "advances in … risk capabilities are compounding" are the bull evidence; provisions outgrowing the book are the bear evidence. The Q3 print is the next data point.

From growth-at-all-costs to disciplined reinvestment
EMERGING TAILWIND

Sea spent 2022–23 proving it could turn a profit and 2024–25 proving the profits could scale (group adjusted EBITDA $1.96B → $3.44B). 2026 is the first year it has chosen to reinvest from a position of profitability rather than from Garena's cash, with a management-set floor on Shopee EBITDA. The market punished that choice in March. The bull framing: a founder-controlled company with net cash, reinvesting at 25%+ GMV growth into a rising take rate, deserves a growth multiple. The bear framing: founder control is exactly why the 2021 overexpansion happened, and "reinvestment" is the same word used then. How to decide: segment EBITDA growth converging back toward revenue growth over the next four quarters. It was 10.6% vs 48.1% in Q2.
