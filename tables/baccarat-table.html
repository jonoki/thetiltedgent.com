Baccarat Trainer: two hands, one rulebook, and the price of every bet.

An 8-deck shoe, the full drawing rulebook and play money. You pick a side and the cards do the rest: nobody makes a decision once the bets are down, which is why there's nothing here to grade. What you do choose is which bets go down and how big, and the table prices every one before the cards come out. Nothing here costs money, which makes it the right place to find out what the Tie costs.

How to use it
1. Put a chip on Banker or Player and deal. 2. Watch the third-card rules play out. The table says why each hand drew or stood. 3. Add a Tie or a pair bet and watch what it does to Your bets. 4. Open the Session tab: what you should have lost, what you did, and how much was luck.
The Baccarat Trainer needs JavaScript. Every bet's house edge is on the baccarat page.
The fine print, baccarat edition. A practice table, not a strategy. No betting pattern, scorecard or progression changes the house edge; the cards don't remember the last hand. Every figure here is exact game math for an 8-deck shoe, counted over every possible deal. Education and entertainment only; no operator is named or endorsed; if gambling stops being fun, that's the game telling you something.
