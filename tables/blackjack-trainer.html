The Practice Room

Blackjack Trainer: learn the chart, then learn to count.

A simulated table with real rules, real shuffles and a dealer who never gets tired. Pick the number of decks and the house rules, seat a few other players so the cards come out the way they do in a casino, and set the deal speed. Play mode grades every decision against basic strategy (or the index plays, once you're counting). Count drill auto-plays every seat so all you do is keep the running count — then check it against the truth whenever you like. Nothing here costs money, which is the only reason it's the right place to learn.

How to use it
1. Play with "show advice" on until the chart is in your hands. 2. Turn advice off; aim for 95%+ accuracy in the Stats tab. 3. Switch to the count drill at a slow speed with the live count showing. 4. Hide the count, speed it up, check yourself every few hands. 5. Turn on index plays and play for real (well, for nothing).
The trainer needs JavaScript. The strategy chart and the counting guide are on the blackjack page.
The fine print, practice edition. This is a training tool, not a promise. Basic strategy cuts the house edge to about half a percent; it does not beat the game. Counting can, on paper, with a big bet spread, deep penetration, a 3:2 table and a casino that hasn't noticed you — and the variance is brutal even then. The trainer exists so you understand the game you're paying for, not so you go looking for a fight with a surveillance department. Education and entertainment only; no operator is named or endorsed; if gambling stops being fun, that's the game telling you something.
