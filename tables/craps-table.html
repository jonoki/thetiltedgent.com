Craps Table: learn the layout, see the price of every bet.

A full craps layout with fair dice and play money. Put chips anywhere on the felt and the table tells you what the bet pays, what the true odds are, and what the house keeps, exactly, to the fraction. Nobody beats the dice, so there's nothing here to grade: the one choice that matters is which bets you make, and every one is priced before you make it. Nothing here costs money, which is the only reason it's the right place to learn.

How to use it
1. Put a chip on the Pass line and roll. 2. When a point is set, take the odds behind your bet. They're the only bet with no house edge. 3. Try a come bet, a place bet, the field. Read each bet card first. 4. Open the Session tab: what you should have lost, what you did, and how much was luck.
The craps table needs JavaScript. Every bet's house edge is on the craps page.
The fine print, craps edition. A practice table, not a strategy. No bet, betting pattern or progression changes the house edge on the dice; the odds bet doesn't lower it, it only adds money the house has no edge on. Every figure here is exact game math for the rules you picked. Education and entertainment only; no operator is named or endorsed; if gambling stops being fun, that's the game telling you something.
