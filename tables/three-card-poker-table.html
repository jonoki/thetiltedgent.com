The Practice Room

Three Card Poker Table: one decision, priced on every hand.

Three cards for you, three for the dealer, and one choice: Play or fold. Most players make it by feel. Here every hand comes with its exact price: what playing is worth, what folding costs, and what the Queen-Six-Four rule would do. The house keeps its edge whatever you choose. What you choose decides how much more it keeps. Nothing here costs money, which is the only reason it's the right place to learn.

How to use it
1. Put a chip on the Ante and deal. 2. Look at your cards and the two prices under the felt, then Play or fold. 3. Try Pair Plus beside the Ante. Read its bet card first, then check the flush line. 4. Open the Session tab: what you should have lost, what you did, how much was the cards and how much was you.
The Three Card Poker table needs JavaScript. Every bet's house edge is on the Three Card Poker page.
The fine print, Three Card Poker edition. A practice table, not a strategy. Correct play keeps the Ante's house edge at its minimum; it doesn't remove it, and no betting pattern does. Every figure here is exact game math for the paytables you picked, counted over every hand you and the dealer can hold.
